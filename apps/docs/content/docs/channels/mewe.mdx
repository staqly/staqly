---
title: MeWe
description: Publish to your MeWe timeline or your MeWe groups with text and pictures. What to register in MeWe's developer program.
---

Staqly publishes to **MeWe** through MeWe's Open API (a developer preview): the person signs in on
mewe.com, and staqly posts to their own timeline (`POST /api/dev/me/post`) or to one of their groups
(`POST /api/dev/group/{groupId}/post`), with pictures uploaded first. Each timeline or group is its
own channel. A published target links to the timeline or the group: MeWe's API does not say the new
post's address.

## What you need

A **MeWe app** of the **Standalone** type, registered once per server. MeWe's developer program is
a **beta with limited places**: MeWe reviews who joins and reviews each app. Its keys go into the
server's environment (`.env`), the same way locally and in the cloud (see
[Developer apps](/channels/developer-apps)); a workspace can also use its own app.

<Steps>
<Step>

### Join the developer program and create the app

Sign in at [dev.mewe.com/developer](https://dev.mewe.com/developer) with a MeWe account and apply
to become a developer. Once MeWe approves you, create an application:

| Where | What |
|---|---|
| **Type** | Standalone |
| **Permissions** | `user_profile` (read the person), `user_groups` (list their groups and post to them), `post_content` (post and upload pictures) |
| **Redirect URI** | your dashboard's `/channels/callback/mewe`, for example `http://localhost:8080/channels/callback/mewe` locally or `https://staqly.example.com/channels/callback/mewe` |

Copy the **App ID** and the **API key** from the app's developer settings.

</Step>
<Step>

### Give staqly the keys

Put them in the server's environment and restart it:

```bash title=".env"
PROVIDER_MEWE_CLIENT_ID=your-app-id
PROVIDER_MEWE_CLIENT_SECRET=your-api-key
```

- **Docker:** `docker run ... --env-file .env ghcr.io/staqly/staqly:latest`, or `-e PROVIDER_MEWE_CLIENT_ID=...`.
- **Binary:** export both in your shell, then run `staqly serve` or `staqly service install` (the
  service keeps `PROVIDER_*` settings).

Until both are set, MeWe shows as **soon** in **Channels → Add channel**.
`PROVIDER_MEWE_ENABLED=false` hides it altogether.

</Step>
<Step>

### Connect the timeline or groups

**Connect with MeWe** opens mewe.com, where the person signs in and allows the app. Staqly then
lists **your timeline** and every group you belong to; pick one or more, and each becomes its own
channel. Connecting the same timeline or group again reconnects the existing channel.

</Step>
</Steps>

## Reconnecting

MeWe's sign-in has an end date and MeWe offers no way to renew it. When it runs out, the channel
turns **Needs reconnect** (an [alert](/automation/alerts) can tell you): open the row's menu and
reconnect it.

## Limits

| | |
|---|---|
| Text | 5,000 characters |
| Media | up to **4 pictures** per post; MeWe's API takes no videos |

## Comments

MeWe's API does not return the new post's id and has no way to comment, so a post's thread is not
sent on MeWe.

## When publishing fails

| MeWe says | Staqly does |
|---|---|
| The sign-in expired or was revoked (401) | Marks the channel **Needs reconnect** and fails the target; reconnect it from the row's menu |
| Rate limit (420 "Enhance Your Calm", 429) or MeWe unavailable (5xx) | Retries through the job queue (after 1, 5, then 15 minutes) |
| A missing app permission (403, it names the permission), a group you may not post in, a group you left (404) | Fails the target with MeWe's reason; add the permission to the app, or reconnect |

MeWe has no way to recognize a post sent twice. When its answer is lost, staqly does not post again
and fails the target asking you to check the timeline or group first.
