Staqly publishes to **LinkedIn company pages** (and showcase pages) through LinkedIn's REST API:
a page admin signs in, picks the pages, and each becomes its own channel that posts as the page.
A published target links to the post on linkedin.com. To post on your own profile, use
[LinkedIn](/channels/linkedin).

## What you need

A **LinkedIn developer app** with the **Community Management API**, registered once per server.
Its keys go into the server's environment (`.env`), the same way locally and in the cloud (see
[Developer apps](/channels/developer-apps)).

<Steps>
<Step>

### Create the app and request the Community Management API

In the [LinkedIn developer portal](https://www.linkedin.com/developers/apps), click
**Create app**, name the company page it belongs to and verify it from the page (**Settings →
Verify**). Then:

| Where | What |
|---|---|
| **Products** | Request the **Community Management API**. LinkedIn asks about your company and use case and reviews the request; it grants a Development tier first, then Standard after a second review |
| **Auth → Authorized redirect URLs** | your dashboard's `/channels/callback/linkedin_page`, for example `http://localhost:8080/channels/callback/linkedin_page` locally or `https://staqly.example.com/channels/callback/linkedin_page` |

LinkedIn wants the Community Management API alone on its app, so this is usually a second app,
next to the one for [LinkedIn](/channels/linkedin) profiles. Copy the **Client ID** and **Primary
Client Secret** from the **Auth** tab.

</Step>
<Step>

### Give staqly the keys

Put them in the server's environment and restart it:

```bash title=".env"
PROVIDER_LINKEDIN_PAGE_CLIENT_ID=your-client-id
PROVIDER_LINKEDIN_PAGE_CLIENT_SECRET=your-client-secret
```

- **Docker:** `docker run ... --env-file .env ghcr.io/staqly/staqly:latest`, or `-e PROVIDER_LINKEDIN_PAGE_CLIENT_ID=...`.
- **Binary:** export both in your shell, then run `staqly serve` or `staqly service install` (the
  service keeps `PROVIDER_*` settings).

Until both are set, LinkedIn Page shows as **soon** in **Channels → Add channel**.
`PROVIDER_LINKEDIN_PAGE_ENABLED=false` hides it altogether.

</Step>
<Step>

### Connect the pages

**Connect with LinkedIn Page** opens linkedin.com, where a page admin signs in and allows the app.
Staqly asks for these scopes:

| Scope | Why |
|---|---|
| `r_basicprofile` | Read the member's name |
| `rw_organization_admin` | List the pages the member administers, with names and logos |
| `w_organization_social` | Post, upload pictures and videos and comment as the page |
| `r_organization_social` | Read the page's posts |

Staqly then lists the pages where the member is an **admin** or **content admin** (other roles may
not post); each one you pick becomes its own channel with the page's name, logo and address.
Connecting the same page again reconnects the existing one.

</Step>
</Steps>

## Reconnecting

LinkedIn's sign-in lasts **60 days**. Apps with the Community Management API usually get a refresh
token as well, and staqly renews the sign-in with it for up to a year; without one, the channel
turns **Needs reconnect** after 60 days (an [alert](/automation/alerts) can tell you): reconnect it
from the row's menu. A member who loses the page's admin role can't post as it anymore either.

## Limits

| | |
|---|---|
| Text | 3,000 characters |
| Media | up to **9 pictures** (PNG, JPEG, GIF) **or one video** per post |
| Video | uploaded in the parts LinkedIn asks for; staqly waits until LinkedIn has processed it before posting |

Posts as a page are always public. Hashtags (`#launch`) stay hashtags, and characters LinkedIn
treats as formatting go out as written. On staqly's cloud, posts to Pages count on the plan's
monthly LinkedIn posts with those to profiles; see [LinkedIn](/channels/linkedin#posts-per-month-cloud).

## Comments

A post's [thread](/concepts#threads-comments-under-a-post) goes out as comments under the post,
written as the page, in order and after their delays. Comments take text only.

## When publishing fails

| LinkedIn says | Staqly does |
|---|---|
| The sign-in expired or was revoked (401) | Marks the channel **Needs reconnect** and fails the target; reconnect it from the row's menu |
| Rate limit (429) or LinkedIn unavailable (5xx), media still processing | Retries through the job queue (after 1, 5, then 15 minutes) |
| Text too long, a refused picture, a missing permission or page role (403) | Fails the target with LinkedIn's reason; retrying would not help, so it does not |

When an answer is lost and staqly tries again, LinkedIn refuses the copy as a duplicate and names
the post that is already there; staqly records that post and its link, so a retry never publishes
twice.
