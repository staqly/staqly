title: Instagram (Facebook Business)
description: Publish to an Instagram Business or Creator account linked to a Facebook Page, through Facebook Login. The second Instagram option next to Instagram login.
---

**Instagram (Facebook Business)** connects an Instagram account through Facebook: the person signs in on
facebook.com, and staqly lists the Instagram Business or Creator accounts linked to the Facebook Pages they
manage. It is offered only on a server that uses [Instagram login](/channels/instagram#instagram-login-instead)
for **Instagram (Standalone)**; with Unified login, the one **Instagram** tile covers both kinds of sign-in.

Once connected it works like [Instagram](/channels/instagram): posts, reels, stories and carousels, the thread
as comments, statistics, audience comments and unpublishing, with the same limits and settings.

## What you need

- The **Meta app** that serves [Facebook Pages](/channels/facebook): `PROVIDER_META_CLIENT_ID` and
  `PROVIDER_META_CLIENT_SECRET` (or `PROVIDER_INSTAGRAM_BUSINESS_CLIENT_ID` / `_SECRET` for an app of its own).
- In that app, **Facebook Login** with the redirect URL `https://<your dashboard>/channels/callback/instagram_business`,
  and the permissions `instagram_basic`, `instagram_content_publish`, `instagram_manage_comments`,
  `instagram_manage_insights`, `instagram_manage_contents` (deleting a post when you unpublish it), `pages_show_list`, `pages_read_engagement` and `business_management`.
- An Instagram account set to **Business** or **Creator**, linked to a Facebook Page the person manages.
- [Media storage Instagram can reach](/channels/instagram#what-you-need), as for Instagram.

It shows only with `PROVIDER_INSTAGRAM_BUSINESS_ENABLED=true`; unset or `false` hides it, and `PROVIDER_INSTAGRAM_BUSINESS_MAINTENANCE=true` shows it as **soon** while it is under maintenance.

## The sign-in

**Connect** opens facebook.com; the person allows the permissions and picks the Instagram account (staqly lists
the ones linked to their Pages). staqly keeps the Page's token, which doesn't expire while the app keeps its
permissions. If the person removes the app, the channel turns **Needs reconnect**.
