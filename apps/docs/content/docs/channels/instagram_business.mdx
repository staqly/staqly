description: Publish to Instagram accounts linked to Facebook Pages (pictures, carousels, reels and stories, with collaborators and comments). What to register at Meta for Developers.

Staqly publishes to **Instagram professional accounts linked to a Facebook Page** through the
Instagram Graph API with Facebook Login: the person signs in on facebook.com, staqly lists the
Instagram accounts of their Pages, and each account they pick becomes its own channel. A published
target links to the post on instagram.com.

An account without a Facebook Page connects through the [Instagram](/channels/instagram) channel
instead (Instagram Login). Publishing works the same on both.

## What you need

- A **Meta app** with **Facebook Login for Business** and the **Instagram** product, registered once
  per server. Its keys go into the server's environment (`.env`), the same way locally and in the
  cloud (see [Developer apps](/channels/developer-apps)). The app that serves
  [Facebook Pages](/channels/facebook) can serve this channel too.
- **Media storage Instagram can reach.** Instagram does not take uploads: it downloads every picture
  and video from a link. Staqly hands it a time-limited link to the file in
  [S3-compatible storage](/self-hosting/storage), so a server that keeps media on its own disk
  can't publish to Instagram (the target fails and says why). Locally, the bucket needs an address
  on the internet, for example a tunnel to it set as `S3_PUBLIC_URL`.
- An Instagram account set to **Business** or **Creator** and linked to a Facebook Page (Page
  settings → Linked accounts), and a Facebook account with a role on that Page.

<Steps>
<Step>

### Create the app

At [Meta for Developers](https://developers.facebook.com/apps), click **Create app** and choose
**Business**. Add **Facebook Login for Business** and **Instagram** (API setup with Facebook login),
then set:

| Where | What |
|---|---|
| **Facebook Login for Business → Settings → Valid OAuth Redirect URIs** | your dashboard's `/channels/callback/instagram_business`, for example `https://staqly.example.com/channels/callback/instagram_business`. Meta takes `https://` addresses only, so locally use a tunnel to `localhost:8080` |
| **App roles → Roles** | the people who may connect while the app is in development |

Copy the **App ID** and **App secret** from **App settings → Basic** (the Meta app's own keys, not
the Instagram app ID).

</Step>
<Step>

### Give staqly the keys

Put them in the server's environment and restart it:

```bash title=".env"
PROVIDER_INSTAGRAM_BUSINESS_CLIENT_ID=your-meta-app-id
PROVIDER_INSTAGRAM_BUSINESS_CLIENT_SECRET=your-meta-app-secret
```

- **Docker:** `docker run ... --env-file .env ghcr.io/staqly/staqly:latest`, or `-e PROVIDER_INSTAGRAM_BUSINESS_CLIENT_ID=...`.
- **Binary:** export both in your shell, then run `staqly serve` or `staqly service install` (the
  service keeps `PROVIDER_*` settings).

Until both are set, Instagram Business shows as **soon** in **Channels → Add channel**.
`PROVIDER_INSTAGRAM_BUSINESS_ENABLED=false` hides it altogether. A workspace may instead save its own
app over the API (`PUT /v1/providers/instagram_business/app`).

</Step>
<Step>

### Connect the accounts

**Connect with Facebook** opens facebook.com, where the person signs in, chooses the Pages (and
their Instagram accounts) the app may use and allows it. Staqly asks for these permissions:

| Permission | Why |
|---|---|
| `instagram_basic` | Read the account's username, name and picture |
| `instagram_content_publish` | Publish posts, carousels, reels and stories |
| `instagram_manage_comments` | Post the comments of a post's thread |
| `pages_show_list` | List the Pages the Instagram accounts are linked to |
| `pages_read_engagement` | Read those Pages |
| `business_management` | Also list the Pages a business portfolio owns or manages for clients |

Staqly then lists the Instagram accounts of those Pages: pick one or more, and each becomes a
channel showing the account's name, `@username` and picture. Connecting the same account again
reconnects its channel. Without `instagram_manage_comments` the channel connects, but a post's
comments are not sent on it.

If staqly says none of your Pages has an Instagram account, link one in the Page's settings, or
connect again and choose the right Pages when Facebook asks.

</Step>
<Step>

### Before other people connect

While the app is in development, only people with a role on it can connect. Before your customers
connect their accounts, Meta reviews the app: **App Review** for each permission above (a screen
recording of staqly using it) and **Business Verification** of your company.

</Step>
</Steps>

## The sign-in

Each channel keeps the token of the Page its account is linked to, which does not run out. The
channel turns **Needs reconnect** when the person removes the app, changes their password, loses
their role on the Page, or the account is unlinked from it.

## Limits

| | |
|---|---|
| Text | 2,200 characters; up to 30 hashtags |
| Media | at least one file: up to **10** pictures and videos (a carousel), **one video** for a reel, **one file** for a story |
| Pictures | **JPEG only**, up to 8 MB; feed pictures 4:5 to 1.91:1 (the composer flags a picture that doesn't fit) |
| Posts | Instagram takes up to **100 posts a day** per account through its API |

## Settings per post

| Setting | What it does |
|---|---|
| **Post as** (`post_type`) | `post` (default; several files make a carousel, a single video goes out as a reel), `reel` or `story` (stories take no caption) |
| **Also show the reel in the feed** (`share_to_feed`) | Reels only; on by default |
| **Collaborators** (`collaborators`) | Feed posts: up to 3 usernames invited as collaborators; the post shows on their profiles once they accept |

Over the API: `{"post_type": "reel", "share_to_feed": "false"}` in the target's `settings`.

## Comments

A post's [thread](/concepts#threads-comments-under-a-post) goes out as comments under the post, in
order and after their delays: for example a first comment with hashtags. Comments take text only;
stories have none.

## When publishing fails

| Instagram says | Staqly does |
|---|---|
| The sign-in expired or was revoked | Marks the channel **Needs reconnect** and fails the target; reconnect it from the row's menu |
| Rate limit, Instagram unavailable, a media download that timed out | Retries through the job queue (after 1, 5, then 15 minutes) |
| A refused picture (format, size, shape), the daily limit, a restricted account | Fails the target with Instagram's reason; retrying would not help, so it does not |

Instagram has no way to ask "did this post go out?", and a new attempt would make a new post. So
when staqly sent the post but its answer never came, the target fails with "may have published"
instead of trying again: check the profile before you post it again.
