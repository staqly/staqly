Staqly publishes to **Instagram professional accounts** (Business or Creator) through the Instagram
API with Unified Login: the person signs in with Instagram or Facebook, shares one or more
Instagram accounts with the app, and each account they pick becomes its own channel. No Facebook
Page is needed. A published target links to the post on instagram.com.

## What you need

- A **Meta app** owned by a business portfolio, with the **Instagram** use case and **Facebook
  Login for Business**, registered once per server. Its keys and its login configuration go into
  the server's environment, the same way locally and in the cloud (see
  [Developer apps](/channels/developer-apps)). The app that serves [Facebook Pages](/channels/facebook)
  can serve Instagram too.
- **Media storage Instagram can reach.** Instagram does not take uploads: it downloads every picture
  and video from a link. Staqly hands it a time-limited link to the file in
  [S3-compatible storage](/self-hosting/storage), so a server that keeps media on its own disk
  can't publish to Instagram (the target fails and says why). Locally, the bucket needs an address
  on the internet, for example a tunnel to it set as `S3_PUBLIC_URL`.
- An Instagram account set to **Business** or **Creator** (Instagram app → Settings → Account type).
  Personal accounts can't publish through the API.

<Steps>
<Step>

### Create the app and its login configuration

At [Meta for Developers](https://developers.facebook.com/apps), click **Create app**, choose a
**Business** app and add the use case **Manage messaging & content on Instagram**. Open it
(**Customize**), then **API setup with Unified login**:

| Where | What |
|---|---|
| **Configure your app → Create new configuration** | a configuration whose access token **never expires**, with the Instagram account as its asset and the permissions below. Copy its **configuration ID** |
| **Redirect URL** | your dashboard's `/channels/callback/instagram`, for example `https://staqly.example.com/channels/callback/instagram`. Meta takes `https://` addresses only, so locally use a tunnel to `localhost:8080` |
| **App roles → Roles** | the people who may connect while the app is in development |

Copy the **App ID** and **App secret** from **App settings → Basic** (the Meta app's own keys).

</Step>
<Step>

### Give staqly the keys

Put them in the server's environment and restart it:

```bash title=".env"
PROVIDER_META_CLIENT_ID=your-meta-app-id
PROVIDER_META_CLIENT_SECRET=your-meta-app-secret
PROVIDER_INSTAGRAM_CONFIG_ID=your-configuration-id
```

- **Docker:** `docker run ... --env-file .env ghcr.io/staqly/staqly:latest`, or `-e PROVIDER_META_CLIENT_ID=...`.
- **Binary:** export all three in your shell, then run `staqly serve` or `staqly service install` (the
  service keeps `PROVIDER_*` settings). [Facebook Page](/channels/facebook) uses the same two keys.

Until the ID and secret are set, Instagram shows as **soon** in **Channels → Add channel**; without
the configuration ID, connecting says it is missing. `PROVIDER_INSTAGRAM_ENABLED=false` hides
Instagram altogether. A workspace may instead save its own app over the API
(`PUT /v1/providers/instagram/app` with `client_id`, `client_secret` and `config_id`).

</Step>
<Step>

### Connect the accounts

**Connect** opens facebook.com, where the person signs in with Instagram or Facebook, chooses the
Instagram accounts to share and allows the app. The configuration asks for these permissions:

| Permission | Why |
|---|---|
| `instagram_basic` | Read the accounts' usernames, names and pictures |
| `instagram_content_publish` | Publish posts, carousels, reels and stories |
| `instagram_manage_comments` | Post the comments of a post's thread |
| `instagram_manage_insights` | Read a post's views, shares and saves (**Statistics**) |

Staqly then lists the shared accounts: pick one or more, and each becomes a channel showing the
account's name, `@username` and picture. Connecting the same account again reconnects its channel.
Without `instagram_manage_comments` the channel connects, but a post's comments are not sent on it.

</Step>
<Step>

### Before other people connect

While the app is in development, only people with a role on it can connect. Before your customers
connect their accounts, Meta reviews the app: **App Review** for each permission above (a screen
recording of staqly using it) and **Business Verification** of your company.

</Step>
</Steps>

## The sign-in

The sign-in gives a token that reaches every account the person shared, and with a configuration
whose tokens never expire it does not run out. The channel turns **Needs reconnect** when the
person removes the app from their business portfolio or moves the account to another portfolio.

## Limits

| | |
|---|---|
| Text | 2,200 characters |
| Media | at least one file: up to **10** pictures and videos (a carousel), **one video** for a reel, **one file** for a story |
| Pictures | **JPEG only**, up to 8 MB, at least 320 pixels wide; feed pictures 4:5 to 1.91:1 (the composer flags a picture that doesn't fit) |

## Settings per post

| Setting | What it does |
|---|---|
| **Post as** (`post_type`) | `post` (default; several files make a carousel, a single video goes out as a reel), `reel` or `story` (stories take no caption) |
| **Also show the reel in the feed** (`share_to_feed`) | Reels only; on by default |
| **Collaborators** (`collaborators`) | Feed posts: up to 3 usernames invited as collaborators; the post shows on their profiles once they accept |

Over the API: `{"post_type": "reel", "share_to_feed": "false"}` in the target's `settings`.

## Comments

A post's [thread](/concepts#threads-comments-under-a-post) goes out as comments under the post, in
order and after their delays: for example a first comment with hashtags. Comments take text only;
stories have none.

## Statistics

A published post's **Statistics** (`GET /v1/posts/{id}/metrics`) show its likes and comments and, with
`instagram_manage_insights` in the configuration, its views, shares and saves. A story's numbers last as
long as the story (a day). An account connected before the configuration had the permission shows likes and
comments until it is reconnected.


## When publishing fails

| Instagram says | Staqly does |
|---|---|
| The sign-in was revoked | Marks the channel **Needs reconnect** and fails the target; reconnect it from the row's menu |
| Rate limit, Instagram unavailable, a media download that timed out | Retries through the job queue (after 1, 5, then 15 minutes) |
| A refused picture (format, size, shape), the daily limit, a restricted account | Fails the target with Instagram's reason; retrying would not help, so it does not |

Instagram has no way to ask "did this post go out?", and a new attempt would make a new post. So
when staqly sent the post but its answer never came, the target fails with "may have published"
instead of trying again: check the profile before you post it again.
