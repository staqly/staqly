title: TikTok

---

Staqly publishes to **TikTok** through the Content Posting API: it signs in with Login Kit
(OAuth 2.0), asks TikTok what the account may post, uploads the video's bytes in parts (no public
link needed) and waits until TikTok reports it published. A published target links to the video
on tiktok.com, or to the profile while TikTok is still reviewing it.

## What you need

A **TikTok developer app** with **Login Kit** and **Content Posting API** (direct post), registered
once per server. Its keys go into the server's environment, the same way locally and in the
cloud; a workspace can also use its own app (see [Developer apps](/channels/developer-apps)).

<Steps>
<Step>

### Create the app

At [developers.tiktok.com](https://developers.tiktok.com/apps), create an app, then:

| Where | What |
|---|---|
| **Products** | Add **Login Kit** and **Content Posting API**; in Content Posting API, turn on **Direct Post** |
| **Login Kit → Redirect URI** | your dashboard's `/channels/callback/tiktok`, for example `https://staqly.example.com/channels/callback/tiktok`. TikTok takes `https://` addresses only, so locally use a tunnel to `localhost:8080` |
| **Scopes** | `user.info.basic`, `user.info.profile`, `video.publish` |

Copy the **Client key** and **Client secret** from the app's page.

</Step>
<Step>

### Give staqly the keys

Put them in the server's environment and restart it:

```bash title=".env"
PROVIDER_TIKTOK_CLIENT_ID=your-client-key
PROVIDER_TIKTOK_CLIENT_SECRET=your-client-secret
```

- **Docker:** `docker run ... --env-file .env ghcr.io/staqly/staqly:latest`, or `-e PROVIDER_TIKTOK_CLIENT_ID=...`.
- **Binary:** export both in your shell, then run `staqly serve` or `staqly service install` (the
  service keeps `PROVIDER_*` settings).

Until both are set, TikTok shows as **soon** in **Channels → Add channel**.
`PROVIDER_TIKTOK_ENABLED=false` hides it altogether.

</Step>
<Step>

### Connect the account

**Connect with TikTok** opens tiktok.com, where the person signs in and allows the app. Staqly asks
for these scopes:

| Scope | Why |
|---|---|
| `user.info.basic` | Read the account's name and picture |
| `user.info.profile` | Read the `@username` the channel shows |
| `video.publish` | Post videos to the account |

TikTok's sign-in lasts a day and renews for a year; staqly renews it on its own.

</Step>
<Step>

### Before other people connect: TikTok's audit

Until TikTok **audits** the app, it publishes **only as "Only you"** (`SELF_ONLY`, private videos),
and only for accounts that are private. Submit the app for review in the developer portal (TikTok
checks that the app follows its [content sharing
guidelines](https://developers.tiktok.com/doc/content-sharing-guidelines)) before posting publicly.
Staqly asks TikTok which audiences each account may use before every post and fails with the
choices when **Who can watch** isn't one of them.

</Step>
</Steps>

## Limits

| | |
|---|---|
| Media | exactly **one video** (MP4, WebM or MOV); pictures are not posted |
| Caption | up to 2,200 characters, hashtags and mentions included |
| Video | 360 to 4,096 pixels on each side, 23 to 60 frames per second; its length up to what TikTok allows the account (often 10 minutes) |
| Posts | TikTok limits how many posts an account and an app make a day |

## Settings per post

| Setting | What it does |
|---|---|
| **Who can watch** (`privacy_level`) | Required on every post, no default (TikTok's rule): `PUBLIC_TO_EVERYONE`, `MUTUAL_FOLLOW_FRIENDS`, `FOLLOWER_OF_CREATOR` or `SELF_ONLY` |
| **Allow comments**, **Allow duets**, **Allow stitches** | On by default; an account that turned one off in TikTok keeps it off |
| **AI-generated content** (`ai_generated`) | TikTok labels the video as AI-generated |
| **Paid partnership** (`branded_content`) | Labels it as promoting a third party; can't be "Only you" |
| **Promotes your own business** (`your_brand`) | Labels it as promotional content |

Over the API: `{"privacy_level": "FOLLOWER_OF_CREATOR", "allow_duet": "false"}` in the target's
`settings`.

## Comments

TikTok's API cannot comment, so a post's thread is not sent to TikTok.

## When publishing fails

| TikTok says | Staqly does |
|---|---|
| The sign-in expired or was revoked | Marks the channel **Needs reconnect** and fails the target; reconnect it from the row's menu |
| Rate limit, TikTok unavailable | Retries through the job queue (after 1, 5, then 15 minutes) |
| An audience the account may not use, the daily limit, a video TikTok can't use (format, length, frame rate), spam | Fails the target with TikTok's reason; retrying would not help, so it does not |

Once the video is uploaded TikTok publishes it on its own, so staqly never uploads it again: if the
job's time runs out while TikTok is still processing, the target fails with "may have published".
Check the profile before you post it again.
