Staqly publishes to your **LinkedIn profile** through LinkedIn's REST API: it signs in with
OAuth 2.0, uploads pictures and videos, and posts with `POST /rest/posts`. A published target
links to the post on linkedin.com. Company pages have their own channel,
[LinkedIn Page](/channels/linkedin_page), which needs an app LinkedIn has reviewed.

## What you need

A **LinkedIn developer app** with two products that LinkedIn grants at once, registered once per
server. Its keys go into the server's environment, the same way locally and in the cloud
(see [Developer apps](/channels/developer-apps)).

<Steps>
<Step>

### Create the app

In the [LinkedIn developer portal](https://www.linkedin.com/developers/apps), click
**Create app**. LinkedIn asks for a company page the app belongs to (any page you manage) and a
logo. Then:

| Where | What |
|---|---|
| **Products** | Request **Share on LinkedIn** and **Sign In with LinkedIn using OpenID Connect**; both are granted right away |
| **Auth → Authorized redirect URLs** | your dashboard's `/channels/callback/linkedin`, for example `http://localhost:8080/channels/callback/linkedin` locally or `https://staqly.example.com/channels/callback/linkedin` |

Copy the **Client ID** and **Primary Client Secret** from the **Auth** tab.

</Step>
<Step>

### Give staqly the keys

Put them in the server's environment and restart it:

```bash title=".env"
PROVIDER_LINKEDIN_CLIENT_ID=your-client-id
PROVIDER_LINKEDIN_CLIENT_SECRET=your-client-secret
```

- **Docker:** `docker run ... --env-file .env ghcr.io/staqly/staqly:latest`, or `-e PROVIDER_LINKEDIN_CLIENT_ID=...`.
- **Binary:** export both in your shell, then run `staqly serve` or `staqly service install` (the
  service keeps `PROVIDER_*` settings).

Until both are set, LinkedIn shows as **soon** in **Channels → Add channel**.
`PROVIDER_LINKEDIN_ENABLED=false` hides it altogether.

</Step>
<Step>

### Connect the account

**Connect with LinkedIn** opens linkedin.com, where the person signs in and allows the app.
Staqly asks for these scopes:

| Scope | Why |
|---|---|
| `openid`, `profile` | Read the member's name and picture |
| `w_member_social` | Publish posts, upload pictures and videos; comments also need the Community Management API |

The channel shows the member's name and picture. Connecting the same account again reconnects the
existing channel.

</Step>
</Steps>

## Reconnecting every 60 days

LinkedIn's sign-in lasts **60 days**, and LinkedIn renews it automatically only for apps in its
partner programs. So after 60 days the channel turns **Needs reconnect** (an
[alert](/automation/alerts) can tell you): open the row's menu and reconnect it, which takes a click
while you are signed in to LinkedIn. Until then, posts to it fail with the reason.

## Limits

| | |
|---|---|
| Text | 3,000 characters |
| Media | up to **9 pictures** (PNG, JPEG, GIF, up to 36,152,320 pixels each) **or one video** per post |
| Video | uploaded in the parts LinkedIn asks for; staqly waits until LinkedIn has processed it before posting |
| Daily quota | LinkedIn counts calls per member and per app for 24 hours from midnight UTC. Posts over it wait and go out after midnight UTC |

Hashtags (`#launch`) stay hashtags, and characters LinkedIn treats as formatting (`( ) [ ] * _`
and others) go out as written.

## Settings per post

| Setting | What it does |
|---|---|
| **Who can see it** (`visibility`) | Anyone (`public`, default) or your connections only (`connections`) |

Over the API: `{"visibility": "connections"}` in the target's `settings`.

## Comments

A post's [thread](/concepts#threads-comments-under-a-post) goes out as comments under the post, in
order and after their delays: for example the link that would otherwise lower the post's reach.
Comments take text only.

LinkedIn lets an app comment only with the `w_member_social_feed` permission of its **Community
Management API**, which LinkedIn reviews; **Share on LinkedIn** (`w_member_social`) only creates
posts, so the post goes out and LinkedIn refuses the comments
(`Not enough permissions to access: partnerApiSocialActions.CREATE`). staqly reads what LinkedIn
allowed when you connect: on a connection without comments, the composer says so before you schedule,
the post goes out and its comments show as not sent. Once the app has the Community Management API,
connect the channel again to turn comments on.

Reading the comments under a post ([audience comments](/dashboard/posts#audience-comments)) needs the same Community Management API.

## Posts per month (cloud)

LinkedIn limits how many posts and video uploads one app can send each day, shared by every
workspace on staqly's cloud, so each plan includes a monthly share: 300, 600, 1,200 or 2,500 posts
to LinkedIn (Starter, Plus, Pro, Business), of which 30, 60, 150 or 300 can have a video. Posts to
a profile and to a [Page](/channels/linkedin_page) count alike, a post with a video counts on both,
and comments don't count. When one runs out, the post is refused with the reason (*Your LinkedIn
posts for this period are used up...*) until the 1st of the next month (UTC); see
[Plans](/plans#how-limits-are-counted). **Settings → Plan & usage** shows both counts. The
Community Edition has no allowance.

## When publishing fails

| LinkedIn says | Staqly does |
|---|---|
| The sign-in expired or was revoked (401) | Marks the channel **Needs reconnect** and fails the target; reconnect it from the row's menu |
| Daily quota reached (429) | Keeps the target **publishing** with the reason and tries again just after midnight UTC, without using up its retries |
| LinkedIn unavailable (5xx), media still processing | Retries through the job queue (after 1, 5, then 15 minutes) |
| Text too long, a refused picture, a missing permission (403) | Fails the target with LinkedIn's reason; retrying would not help, so it does not |

LinkedIn has no way to ask "did this post go out?". When an answer is lost and staqly tries again,
LinkedIn refuses the copy as a duplicate and names the post that is already there; staqly records
that post and its link, so a retry never publishes twice.
