title: Facebook Page
description: Publish posts, pictures, videos, reels and stories to Facebook Pages, with comments. What to register at Meta for Developers and how to pick the Pages.
---

Staqly publishes to **Facebook Pages** through the Graph API with Facebook Login: the person signs
in on facebook.com, staqly lists the Pages they may post to, and each Page they pick becomes its
own channel. Staqly publishes as the Page, never as the person. A published target links to the
post on facebook.com.

## What you need

- A **Meta app** with **Facebook Login for Business**, registered once per server. Its keys go into
  the server's environment, the same way locally and in the cloud (see
  [Developer apps](/channels/developer-apps)).
- A Facebook account with a role on the Page that lets it publish (full control, or partial access
  with content).

Facebook takes uploads, so unlike Instagram and Threads a Facebook Page needs no public media
storage: staqly sends each file's bytes. A video kept in [S3-compatible storage](/self-hosting/storage)
is handed to Facebook as a link instead, which spares the server the upload.

<Steps>
<Step>

### Create the app

At [Meta for Developers](https://developers.facebook.com/apps), click **Create app** and choose
**Business** (or the use case **Manage everything on your Page**). Add **Facebook Login for
Business**, then set:

| Where | What |
|---|---|
| **Facebook Login for Business → Settings → Valid OAuth Redirect URIs** | your dashboard's `/channels/callback/facebook`, for example `https://staqly.example.com/channels/callback/facebook`. Meta takes `https://` addresses only, so locally use a tunnel to `localhost:8080` |
| **App settings → Basic** | a privacy policy URL and a category (Meta asks for both before the app goes live) |
| **App roles → Roles** | the people who may connect while the app is in development |

Copy the **App ID** and **App secret** from **App settings → Basic**.

</Step>
<Step>

### Give staqly the keys

Put them in the server's environment and restart it:

```bash title=".env"
PROVIDER_META_CLIENT_ID=your-meta-app-id
PROVIDER_META_CLIENT_SECRET=your-meta-app-secret
```

- **Docker:** `docker run ... --env-file .env ghcr.io/staqly/staqly:latest`, or `-e PROVIDER_META_CLIENT_ID=...`.
- **Binary:** export both in your shell, then run `staqly serve` or `staqly service install` (the
  service keeps `PROVIDER_*` settings).

Until both are set, Facebook Page shows as **soon** in **Channels → Add channel**.
`PROVIDER_FACEBOOK_ENABLED=false` hides it altogether. The same Meta app, with the same keys, also serves
[Instagram](/channels/instagram) (which adds a login configuration).
A workspace may instead save its own app over the API (`PUT /v1/providers/facebook/app`).

</Step>
<Step>

### Connect the Pages

**Connect with Facebook** opens facebook.com, where the person signs in, chooses the Pages the app
may use and allows it. Staqly asks for these permissions:

| Permission | Why |
|---|---|
| `pages_show_list` | List the Pages the person manages |
| `business_management` | Also list the Pages a business portfolio owns or manages for clients |
| `pages_manage_posts` | Publish posts, reels and stories as the Page |
| `pages_read_engagement` | Read the Page's name, picture and posts, and a post's reactions, comments and shares (**Statistics**) |
| `pages_manage_engagement` | Post the comments of a post's thread as the Page |

Staqly then lists the Pages: pick one or more, and each becomes a channel showing the Page's name,
`@username` (or its id, for a Page without one) and picture. A Page staqly finds through a business
portfolio but on which the person has no role is left out. Connecting the same Page again
reconnects its channel.

Without `pages_manage_engagement` the channel connects, but a post's comments are not sent on it.

</Step>
<Step>

### Before other people connect

While the app is in development, only people with a role on it can connect. Before your customers
connect their Pages, Meta reviews the app: **App Review** for each permission above (a screen
recording of staqly using it) and **Business Verification** of your company.

</Step>
</Steps>

## The sign-in

The person's sign-in is traded for a 60-day token, and each Page channel keeps the **Page's own
token**, which does not run out. The channel turns **Needs reconnect** when the person removes the
app, changes their password, or loses their role on the Page.

## Limits

| | |
|---|---|
| Text | 63,206 characters |
| Media | up to **10 pictures**, or **one video**: not both in one post |
| Pictures | JPEG, PNG, GIF, BMP, TIFF or WebP, up to 10 MB |
| Reels | one video |
| Stories | one picture or one video; no text |

## Settings per post

| Setting | What it does |
|---|---|
| **Post as** (`post_type`) | `post` (default: text, pictures or a video in the feed), `reel` or `story` |

Over the API: `{"post_type": "reel"}` in the target's `settings`.

## Comments

A post's [thread](/concepts#threads-comments-under-a-post) goes out as comments under the post, as
the Page, in order and after their delays. A comment takes text and at most one picture. Stories
take no comments.

## Unpublishing

Unpublishing deletes the post on the Page through the API, thread comments first.

## Statistics

A published post's **Statistics** (`GET /v1/posts/{id}/metrics`) show its reactions (as likes), comments and
shares (videos and reels have no shares count). They need no extra permission.

[Audience comments](/dashboard/posts#audience-comments) also need `pages_read_user_content`, which the Meta app must have approved through App Review: without it Meta may hide commenters' names or text.


## When publishing fails

| Facebook says | Staqly does |
|---|---|
| The sign-in expired or was revoked, the person lost their role on the Page | Marks the channel **Needs reconnect** and fails the target; reconnect it from the row's menu |
| Rate limit, posting too fast, Facebook unavailable | Retries through the job queue (after 1, 5, then 15 minutes) |
| A duplicate post, a refused picture or video, a link to facebook.com in the post, a Community Standards block, a Page blocked from posting for a while, a security check on the account | Fails the target with Facebook's reason; retrying would not help, so it does not |

Facebook has no way to ask "did this post go out?", and a new attempt would make a new post. So
when staqly sent the post but its answer never came, the target fails with "may have published"
instead of trying again: check the Page before you post it again.
