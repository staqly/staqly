description: Publish posts with pictures or a video, content warnings and replies to any Mastodon server. Sign in with Mastodon, or connect with an access token.

Staqly publishes to a **Mastodon** account, or to one on any server that speaks Mastodon's API, such as
GoToSocial or Akkoma. A published target links to the post on your server. There are two ways to connect, and
nothing for whoever runs staqly to register:

- **Sign in with Mastodon** (OAuth): the usual way.
- **An access token** you make on your server: when your server's sign-in doesn't work with staqly.

## Sign in with Mastodon

In **Channels → Add channel → Mastodon**, enter your **Server** (for example `mastodon.social`, the default)
and choose **Connect with Mastodon**. Your server's sign-in page opens; sign in and choose **Authorize**. You
come back with the account connected: its display name, `@username@server` and picture.

Mastodon apps belong to one server each, so staqly registers itself on your server as the sign-in starts
(an application named **staqly**, with the scopes `write:statuses`, `write:media` and `read:accounts`). It
returns to `<your dashboard>/channels/callback/mastodon`, so the sign-in works on a local install too. Staqly
keeps the account's token sealed; Mastodon's tokens don't expire. Removing staqly in **Preferences →
Account → Authorized apps** disconnects it; the channel then shows **Needs reconnect**.

## With an access token

<Steps>
<Step>

### Make an access token

On your Mastodon server, open **Preferences → Development → New application**:

| Field | What |
|---|---|
| **Application name** | anything, for example `staqly` |
| **Redirect URI** | leave the default, `urn:ietf:wg:oauth:2.0:oob` (staqly doesn't use it) |
| **Scopes** | untick everything, then tick `write:statuses`, `write:media` and `read:accounts` |

Save, open the application and copy **Your access token**.

</Step>
<Step>

### Connect the account

In **Channels → Add channel → Mastodon**, choose **Use an access token instead** and enter:

| Field | What |
|---|---|
| **Server** | your server's address, for example `https://mastodon.social` (the default) |
| **Access token** | the token from the previous step |

The channel shows your display name, `@username@server` and picture. Staqly keeps the token sealed.

</Step>
</Steps>

Over the API, this is the provider `mastodon_token` (`POST /v1/channels` with the fields `instance` and
`access_token`); `mastodon` signs in from the dashboard.

## Limits

| | |
|---|---|
| Text | 500 characters (Mastodon's default; staqly keeps to 500 even on servers that allow more) |
| Media | up to **4 pictures**, or **one video** alone |

Your server processes a video before the post goes out; a long video makes the publish wait for it.

## Settings per post

| Setting | What it does |
|---|---|
| **Who can see it** (`visibility`) | `public` (default), `unlisted` (quiet public: not in public timelines) or `private` (followers only) |
| **Content warning** (`spoiler_text`) | Hides the post (and marks its media sensitive) behind this text until a reader opens it |

Over the API: `{"visibility": "unlisted", "spoiler_text": "Spoilers for episode 3"}` in the target's `settings`.

## Replies

A post's [thread](/concepts#threads-comments-under-a-post) goes out as replies, each under the one
before, with the post's visibility and content warning.

## When publishing fails

| Mastodon says | Staqly does |
|---|---|
| The token is invalid, revoked or lacks a scope | Marks the channel **Needs reconnect** and fails the target; sign in again (or make a new token) and reconnect |
| Rate limit, the server is down or can't be reached | Retries through the job queue (after 1, 5, then 15 minutes) |
| A refused post (too long, a file the server doesn't take, a deleted post replied to) | Fails the target with Mastodon's reason |

Every post is sent with an **idempotency key**: when its answer is lost, the next attempt gets the
post Mastodon already made instead of a second one. Mastodon remembers the key for an hour, so
retrying a failed target by hand much later could post twice: check the profile first.
