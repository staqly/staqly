---
title: Nostr
description: Publish text notes and replies to Nostr relays, signed with your private key.
---

Staqly publishes **text notes** to Nostr: it signs each note with your private key and sends it to
your relays. There is nothing for whoever runs staqly to register. A published target links to the
note on njump.me.

## Connect the account

In **Channels → Add channel → Nostr**, enter:

| Field | What |
|---|---|
| **Private key** | your `nsec1...` key (or its 64-character hex form), from your Nostr app's key or account settings |
| **Relays** | the relays your Nostr app writes to, comma-separated `wss://` addresses (default `wss://relay.damus.io, wss://nos.lol, wss://relay.primal.net`) |

The private key is your account: anyone who has it can post as you. Staqly keeps it sealed and uses
it only to sign notes. If you'd rather not hand over your main key, make a separate Nostr account
for publishing.

The channel shows your `npub`, and your name and picture when one of the relays has your profile.
Connecting fails when none of the relays can be reached.

## Limits

| | |
|---|---|
| Text | no limit |
| Media | none: Nostr channels publish text only (pictures need an upload server staqly doesn't ask for yet) |

`#hashtags` in the text are added as tags, so Nostr apps list the note under them.

## Settings per post

None.

## Replies

A post's [thread](/concepts#threads-comments-under-a-post) goes out as replies, each under the one
before, marked as part of the post's thread.

## When publishing fails

A note is published when **at least one relay** accepts it.

| Relays say | Staqly does |
|---|---|
| None can be reached, or all are rate limiting | Retries through the job queue (after 1, 5, then 15 minutes) |
| All refuse the note (for example a relay that takes paying members only) | Fails the target with each relay's reason; change your relays and reconnect |
| The private key stored is not valid | Marks the channel **Needs reconnect** |

A note's id includes the moment it was signed, so a new attempt is a new note. When a relay took the
note but never answered, the target fails with "may have published" instead of trying again: check
your profile before you post it again.
