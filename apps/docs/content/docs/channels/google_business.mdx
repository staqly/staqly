---
title: Google Business Profile
description: Publish updates with a photo and a button to a Google Business Profile location (formerly Google My Business). What to register in the Google Cloud console and what Google must approve.
---

Staqly publishes **updates** to a **Google Business Profile** location (formerly Google My
Business): the post shows on the business's profile in Google Search and Maps. The person signs in
with Google, picks one or more locations, and each becomes its own channel. A published target
links to the post on Google.

## What you need

- A **Google Cloud project** approved for the **Business Profile APIs** and an OAuth client,
  registered once per server. Its keys go into the server's environment (`.env`), the same way
  locally and in the cloud (see [Developer apps](/channels/developer-apps)). The project and
  client of [YouTube](/channels/youtube) can serve here too.
- **Media storage Google can reach**, for posts with a photo. Google does not take uploads: it
  downloads the photo from a link. Staqly hands it a time-limited link to the file in
  [S3-compatible storage](/self-hosting/storage), so a server that keeps media on its own disk
  publishes text posts only (a post with a photo fails and says why).
- A Google account that is an owner or manager of the business, and a **verified** location.

<Steps>
<Step>

### Ask Google for API access

Google opens the Business Profile APIs to approved projects only: until then every call is refused
(its quota is 0 requests a minute). Create the Cloud project, then request access with the
[Business Profile API access form](https://developers.google.com/my-business/content/prereqs);
Google answers by email, usually within a few days. Once approved, enable these APIs in
**APIs & Services → Library**:

- **My Business Account Management API** (lists the business accounts)
- **My Business Business Information API** (lists the locations)
- **Google My Business API** (publishes the posts)

</Step>
<Step>

### Create the OAuth client

In the [Google Cloud console](https://console.cloud.google.com/):

| Where | What |
|---|---|
| **Google Auth Platform → Branding / Audience** | Fill in the app's name and support address; while the app is in **Testing**, add the Google accounts that may connect as **Test users** |
| **Google Auth Platform → Data access** | Add the scopes listed below |
| **Google Auth Platform → Clients → Create client** | Type **Web application**; under **Authorized redirect URIs** add your dashboard's `/channels/callback/google_business`, for example `http://localhost:8080/channels/callback/google_business` locally or `https://staqly.example.com/channels/callback/google_business` |

Google takes `http://` return addresses for `localhost` only; any other address must be `https://`.
Copy the **Client ID** and **Client secret** of the client.

</Step>
<Step>

### Give staqly the keys

Put them in the server's environment and restart it:

```bash title=".env"
PROVIDER_GOOGLE_BUSINESS_CLIENT_ID=your-client-id.apps.googleusercontent.com
PROVIDER_GOOGLE_BUSINESS_CLIENT_SECRET=your-client-secret
```

- **Docker:** `docker run ... --env-file .env ghcr.io/staqly/staqly:latest`, or `-e PROVIDER_GOOGLE_BUSINESS_CLIENT_ID=...`.
- **Binary:** export both in your shell, then run `staqly serve` or `staqly service install` (the
  service keeps `PROVIDER_*` settings).

Until both are set, Google Business Profile shows as **soon** in **Channels → Add channel**.
`PROVIDER_GOOGLE_BUSINESS_ENABLED=false` hides it altogether. The channel setup check asks Google
whether it takes the keys and whether media links are public.

</Step>
<Step>

### Connect the locations

**Connect with Google Business Profile** opens Google's sign-in. Staqly asks for these scopes:

| Scope | Why |
|---|---|
| `openid`, `email`, `profile` | Read who signed in |
| `business.manage` | List the business accounts and locations, and publish posts |

Staqly then lists every location of every business account the person manages, as "name, town";
each one you pick becomes its own channel. Connecting the same location again reconnects the
existing one.

While the app is in **Testing**, Google signs test users out after **7 days** (the channel turns
**Needs reconnect**); publish the app (**Audience → Publish app**) to keep sign-ins. `business.manage`
is a sensitive scope, so Google verifies the app before people outside your organization see it
without a warning.

</Step>
</Steps>

## Limits

| | |
|---|---|
| Text | required, up to 1,500 characters; Google refuses posts with phone numbers in the text |
| Media | up to **one picture**, JPEG or PNG, up to 5 MB and at least 250 pixels wide; no videos |

## Settings per post

| Setting | What it does |
|---|---|
| **Button** (`call_to_action`) | `none` (default), `LEARN_MORE`, `BOOK`, `ORDER`, `SHOP` (Buy), `SIGN_UP` or `CALL` (dials the profile's phone number) |
| **Button link** (`call_to_action_link`) | Where the button leads; every button but `CALL` needs one |

Over the API: `{"call_to_action": "BOOK", "call_to_action_link": "https://example.com/book"}` in
the target's `settings`.

Google Business Profile posts have no comments, so a post's thread is not sent on it.

## When publishing fails

| Google says | Staqly does |
|---|---|
| The sign-in expired or was revoked | Marks the channel **Needs reconnect** and fails the target; reconnect it from the row's menu |
| Rate limit, Google unavailable | Retries through the job queue (after 1, 5, then 15 minutes) |
| The post breaks a content policy, an invalid button link, a location that is gone or not verified | Fails the target with Google's reason; retrying would not help, so it does not |
| "Google approves each app" when connecting | The project is not approved for the Business Profile APIs yet (see the first step) |

Google has no way to ask "did this post go out?", and a new attempt would make a new post. So
when staqly sent the post but its answer never came, the target fails with "may have published"
instead of trying again: check the business profile before you post it again.
