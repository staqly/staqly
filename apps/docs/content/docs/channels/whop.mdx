Staqly publishes to a **forum of your Whop business** through Whop's REST API, signed in
with Whop (or with the business's Account API key), uploads pictures and videos to Whop, and posts with
`POST /api/v1/forum_posts`. Each forum is its own channel. A published target links to the
business's page on whop.com (Whop's API gives no address for a single post).

## Sign in with Whop

**Channels → Add channel → Whop** sends you to Whop, where you pick the business and approve staqly's
Whop app. The business's forums come back as a list: pick one or more, each becomes a channel, and
they share the sign-in (Whop renews it every hour and rotates its refresh token, so staqly keeps one
for all of them).

This needs a Whop app on the server, registered once:

<Steps>
<Step>

### Register the app

On [whop.com/dashboard](https://whop.com/dashboard), **Developer → Apps → Create app**, type **B2B
app**. Under **OAuth**: client mode **Confidential**, and the redirect URL your dashboard's
`/channels/callback/whop` (`https://staqly.example.com/channels/callback/whop`). Under
**Permissions**, add as required:

| Permission | Why |
|---|---|
| `company:basic:read` | Show the business's name and logo |
| `forum:read` | List the forums to post into |
| `forum:post:create` | Publish posts and comments, with their files |
| `developer:manage_webhook` | Make the channel's webhook (see [Webhook](#webhook)) |
| `webhook_receive:memberships`, `member:basic:read` | New members and memberships in **Analytics → Audience** |

</Step>
<Step>

### Give staqly the keys

```bash title=".env"
PROVIDER_WHOP_ENABLED=true
PROVIDER_WHOP_CLIENT_ID=app_...
PROVIDER_WHOP_CLIENT_SECRET=...
```

Until both are set, **Whop** connects with an API key only.

</Step>
</Steps>

## Or connect with an Account API key

Under the sign-in, **Use an Account API key instead** opens **Whop (API key)**: nothing is
registered on the server, and each forum brings the business's key.

<Steps>
<Step>

### Create the key

On [whop.com/dashboard](https://whop.com/dashboard), pick the business, open the **Developer**
page and, under **Account API keys**, click **Create**. Give it a name (for example `staqly`) and
the permissions in the table above. Copy the key: Whop shows it once.

</Step>
<Step>

### Connect a forum

Paste the key. When the business has one forum, staqly uses it; with several, it lists them
(`General (exp_...)`) and asks for the **Forum** id. To connect another forum, add the channel again
with the same key and the other id. The channel shows the business's name and logo and the forum's
name.

</Step>
</Steps>

A key does not expire. If you delete it on Whop, or a sign-in is revoked (the app removed from the
business), the channel turns **Needs reconnect**.

## Webhook

Once the channel connects (and again daily), staqly makes a webhook on Whop for it, pointed at
`https://<your API>/v1/whop/webhook/<channel id>` (`PUBLIC_API_URL`; Whop refuses `localhost` and
private addresses), and keeps its signing secret with the channel's sign-in or key. Whop signs every event
with it; staqly refuses a call more than five minutes off and handles each one once. A webhook Whop
turned off after failures is turned on again, a deleted one is made again.

- Someone joining the business (`member.created`) and a membership starting
  (`membership.activated`) count in **Analytics → Audience** as new members and new memberships.
- Whop sends nothing for forum posts; staqly reads their numbers and comments instead (see below).

Without the webhook permissions publishing works as before; the Audience stays empty. Two forums of
one business each have a webhook, so the business's members count once per connected forum.

## Limits

| | |
|---|---|
| Text | 5,000 characters, Markdown |
| Media | up to **4** pictures or videos per post, uploaded to Whop first; staqly waits until Whop has processed each one |

## Settings per post

| Setting | What it does |
|---|---|
| **Title** (`title`) | The post's headline in the forum (optional, up to 200 characters). It is the post's **Title**, shared by every channel, not set per channel |
| **Notify members** (`notify`) | Whop sends a mention notification to the forum's members who allow them |
| **Pin to the top** (`pinned`) | Pins the post in the forum |

Over the API: `{"title": "Week 42", "notify": "true"}` in the target's `settings`.

## Comments

A post's [thread](/concepts#threads-comments-under-a-post) goes out as comments, each answering the
one before it, in order and after their delays. Comments take pictures and videos too.

## Numbers and comments

**Insights** reads a published post's views, likes and comments from Whop, and the comments under it with their
first replies (the `forum:read` permission). A sign-in reads the forum it posted to; posts published before
staqly kept the forum with the post read their numbers only.

## When publishing fails

| Whop says | Staqly does |
|---|---|
| The key is not valid (401) | Marks the channel **Needs reconnect**; reconnect it with a working key |
| A missing permission or a forum where only admins post (403) | Fails the target with Whop's reason; give the key **Create forum posts** |
| The forum is gone (404), a refused post (400, 422), a file Whop could not process | Fails the target with Whop's reason |
| Rate limit (429) or Whop unavailable (5xx), an upload that did not go through | Retries through the job queue (after 1, 5, then 15 minutes) |

Whop has no way to recognize a post sent twice. When its answer is lost, staqly does not post again
and fails the target asking you to check the forum first.
