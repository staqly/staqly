---
title: Hashnode
description: Publish articles to a Hashnode blog with a personal access token, with tags, a subtitle, a cover picture and an original link.
---

Staqly publishes **articles** to a [Hashnode](https://hashnode.com) blog through Hashnode's
GraphQL API, signed in with a **personal access token**. The post's text is the article in
Markdown and its **Title** setting the headline. A published target links to the article on the
blog.

## Connect the blog

1. On hashnode.com, open **Account settings → Developer** and click **Generate new token**.
2. In staqly: **Channels → Add channel → Hashnode**, paste it as **Personal access token**.
3. **Blog**: leave it empty when the account has one blog. With several, enter the blog's address
   (`blog.example.com` or `janedoe.hashnode.dev`) or its id; staqly lists the account's blogs when
   it needs you to choose. Connect once per blog to publish to several.

The channel shows the blog's name and address.

## Limits

| | |
|---|---|
| Text | no limit; Markdown |
| Media | one picture, used as the article's **cover** |
| Title | required |

Hashnode takes no uploads: it downloads the cover from a link. Staqly hands it a time-limited link
to the file in [S3-compatible storage](/self-hosting/storage), so on a server that keeps media on
its own disk a post with a picture fails and says why (publish it without the picture, or store
media in the cloud).

## Settings per post

| Setting | What it does |
|---|---|
| **Title** (`title`) | The article's headline (required) |
| **Subtitle** (`subtitle`) | Shown under the title, up to 150 characters |
| **Tags** (`tags`) | Up to 5 tags, by name: `Web Development` is the tag `web-development` |
| **Original link** (`canonical_url`) | Where the article first appeared, so search engines credit it |

## When publishing fails

| Hashnode says | Staqly does |
|---|---|
| The token was revoked or is invalid | Marks the channel **Needs reconnect**; generate a new token and reconnect |
| Rate limit, Hashnode unavailable | Retries through the job queue (after 1, 5, then 15 minutes) |
| A refused article (no permission on the blog, a tag it doesn't take) | Fails the target with Hashnode's reason |

Hashnode has no way to ask "did this article go out?", and a new attempt would make a second one.
So when staqly sent the article but its answer never came, the target fails with "may have
published" instead of trying again: check your blog before you post it again.
