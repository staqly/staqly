Staqly publishes to a **Bluesky** account. A published target links to the post on bsky.app. There are two
ways to connect, and nothing for whoever runs staqly to register:

- **Sign in with Bluesky** (OAuth): the usual way. Works with two-factor sign-in on.
- **An app password**: for an account on a server of its own (a PDS other than Bluesky's), or a staqly whose
  dashboard has no https address.

## Sign in with Bluesky

**Channels → Add channel → Bluesky → Connect with Bluesky** opens Bluesky's sign-in page; sign in and allow
staqly. You come back with the account connected: its display name, `@handle` and picture.

Staqly is its own OAuth client here (AT Protocol OAuth has no app to register): its client id is the address
of a document the dashboard serves, `https://<your dashboard>/api/v1/oauth/bluesky/client-metadata.json`, which
Bluesky fetches. So signing in needs the dashboard on a public **https** address (`PUBLIC_DASHBOARD_URL`);
without one, Bluesky connects with an app password only. The sign-in asks for `atproto transition:generic`:
posting, pictures and the account's profile, not direct messages.

The client signs its requests with a key derived from the server's secret key (`DATA_DIR/secret.key` /
`STAQLY_SECRET_KEY`), and every token is bound to a key of the connection's own (DPoP). Staqly renews the
sign-in by itself. Removing staqly in Bluesky (**Settings → Privacy and security → Apps**) disconnects it; the
channel then shows **Needs reconnect**.

Every **Connect with Bluesky** asks Bluesky which account to use, so another account becomes another channel;
picking an account already connected reconnects its channel.

## With an app password

<Steps>
<Step>

### Make an app password

In Bluesky, open **Settings → Privacy and security → App passwords → Add App Password**, name it
(for example `staqly`) and copy the password (`abcd-efgh-ijkl-mnop`). Leave **Allow access to your
direct messages** off. Never use your account password: staqly refuses it.

</Step>
<Step>

### Connect the account

In **Channels → Add channel → Bluesky**, pick **Account on a server of your own? Use an app password** (on a staqly
without an https address, Bluesky asks for an app password straight away) and enter:

| Field | What |
|---|---|
| **Handle** | your handle without the `@` (`janedoe.bsky.social`, or your own domain), or your DID |
| **App password** | the app password from the previous step |
| **Server** | `https://bsky.social` (the default); change it only if your account lives on its own PDS |

Staqly keeps the app password sealed and signs in with it for every publish, so there is no sign-in to
renew. Deleting the app password in Bluesky disconnects staqly. Over the API this is the provider
`bluesky_app_password`; channels connected before Bluesky had OAuth moved there and keep working.

</Step>
</Steps>

## Limits

| | |
|---|---|
| Text | 300 characters |
| Media | up to **4 pictures** (JPEG, PNG, WebP or GIF), **1 MB** each: a larger JPEG, PNG or WebP goes as a smaller JPEG, a GIF over 1 MB is refused; no video |

Links, `@handle` mentions and `#hashtags` in the text become clickable. A mention of a handle that
doesn't exist stays plain text. Pictures go out without alt text, and links without a preview card.

## Settings per post

None.

## Replies

A post's [thread](/concepts#threads-comments-under-a-post) goes out as replies, each under the one
before, all in the same thread.

## When publishing fails

| Bluesky says | Staqly does |
|---|---|
| The sign-in was revoked or can't be renewed, the app password was deleted or is wrong, the account is suspended | Marks the channel **Needs reconnect** and fails the target; reconnect (with an app password: make a new one) |
| Rate limit, Bluesky unavailable or unreachable | Retries through the job queue (after 1, 5, then 15 minutes) |
| A refused post (too long, a picture too large) | Fails the target with Bluesky's reason |

Each post gets a record key made from the post and the channel, and every attempt first looks for
that record. So when an answer is lost, the next attempt finds the post Bluesky already made
instead of posting it twice.

With an app password, Bluesky limits sign-ins (about 30 every 5 minutes and 300 a day per account). Staqly
signs in once per post and once per reply, so a channel with many long threads can reach it; staqly then
retries later. A channel signed in with Bluesky has no such limit.
