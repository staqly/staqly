---
title: Review on your phone
description: Connect your phone to the staqly dashboard with a QR code so you can approve and reject from anywhere in the house.
---

Agents keep producing while you're away from your desk. In this recipe you open the staqly
dashboard to your Wi-Fi and connect your phone with a QR code, so you can approve and reject
from the couch. It takes about a minute, and the phone signs in without a password.

## What you need

- staqly on your computer: `staqly serve` ([install](/quickstart)) or the
  [Docker image](/self-hosting/docker).
- A phone on the same Wi-Fi as that computer.

## Set it up

<Steps>
<Step>

### Find your computer's LAN address

Your phone needs an address it can reach. Look up your computer's IP on the Wi-Fi, for example
`192.168.1.23`:

```bash
ipconfig getifaddr en0    # macOS
hostname -I               # Linux
```

</Step>
<Step>

### Open the dashboard to your network

By default the dashboard only listens on your computer. Open it to the network and tell staqly
its public address, so the QR code points somewhere your phone can reach:

```bash tab="staqly serve"
PUBLIC_DASHBOARD_URL=http://192.168.1.23:8080 staqly serve --expose
```

```bash tab="Docker"
# -p 8080:8080 already publishes the dashboard on your network; recreate the container with the URL
docker rm -f staqly
docker run -d --name staqly ... -e PUBLIC_DASHBOARD_URL=http://192.168.1.23:8080 ghcr.io/staqly/staqly:latest
```

With Docker, use your full command from [Run with Docker](/self-hosting/docker) and add the `-e`
line; the data stays in the volume.

</Step>
<Step>

### Sign in on your computer

Once the dashboard is on the network, **every browser signs in once**, including the one on
your computer. Create a one-time sign-in link and open it in your browser:

```bash tab="staqly serve"
staqly serve connect-link
```

```bash tab="Docker"
docker exec staqly server connect-link
```

The link works once and expires after 5 minutes.

</Step>
<Step>

### Show the QR code

In the dashboard open **Connect a device**: **Settings → Devices**, or <Kbd>⌘</Kbd> <Kbd>K</Kbd>
→ *Connect a device*.

</Step>
<Step>

### Scan it

Point your phone's camera at the code. staqly opens already signed in. Like the link above,
the QR code works once and expires after 5 minutes.

</Step>
<Step>

### Review

The phone Inbox shows **one large card at a time**. Swipe left and right to move between
items; the sticky bar at the bottom has **Reject**, **Approve** and a menu with the actions that
apply (Regenerate, for example, once an item is rejected). After **Approve**, the toast offers **Undo** for
10 seconds before the approval is sent.

</Step>
</Steps>

## How it works

- The phone gets its own browser session. It appears under **Settings → Devices**; revoke it
  there if you lose the phone.
- The API itself stays on `127.0.0.1`. The phone talks only to the dashboard, and agents on
  your computer keep working without keys.

## Troubleshooting

- **The QR code points at `localhost`:** `PUBLIC_DASHBOARD_URL` isn't set. The dialog shows a hint
  when this happens.
- **The phone can't open the link:** check `PUBLIC_DASHBOARD_URL` uses your computer's LAN IP, that
  both devices are on the same network, and that no firewall blocks port `8080`.
- **"Already used or expired":** each link signs in one browser within 5 minutes. Create a new
  QR code.
- **Away from home?** Run staqly in [cloud mode](/self-hosting/cloud-deployment) behind HTTPS
  and connect your phone the same way. Or skip the dashboard and
  [approve from Telegram](/cookbook/approve-from-chat).
