---
title: Review on your phone
description: Approve and reject from the couch — connect your phone with a QR code in under a minute.
---

<Steps>
<Step>

### Give staqly your LAN address

On the computer running staqly, find its IP on your Wi-Fi (e.g. `192.168.1.23`) and set:

```bash
echo "PUBLIC_WEB_URL=http://192.168.1.23:8080" >> .env
docker compose up -d
```

</Step>
<Step>

### Show the QR code

In the dashboard open **Connect a device** (**Settings → Devices**, or <Kbd>⌘</Kbd> <Kbd>K</Kbd>
→ *Connect a device*).

</Step>
<Step>

### Scan it

Point your phone's camera at the code. staqly opens already signed in — the link works once
and expires after 5 minutes.

</Step>
<Step>

### Review

The phone Inbox shows **one large card at a time**. Swipe left and right to move between
items; the sticky bar at the bottom has **Reject**, **Approve** and a menu with custom actions
such as *Regenerate*.

</Step>
</Steps>

The phone appears under **Settings → Devices** — revoke it there if you lose it. The API itself
stays on `127.0.0.1`: the phone talks only to the dashboard.

Away from home? Run staqly in [cloud mode](/docs/self-hosting/cloud-deployment) behind HTTPS
and connect your phone the same way.
