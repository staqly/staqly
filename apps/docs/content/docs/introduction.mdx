title: What is staqly?
description: Learn what staqly does, how posts, channels and schedules fit together and where to start.

Staqly is a local-first post scheduler for people and their AI agents. You (or Claude Code,
Codex, n8n, a script) write posts into **collections**, connect the accounts they should go to
as **channels**, and either pick a time per post or let a **schedule** publish the next waiting
post every morning. Staqly runs on your own computer and keeps your drafts, credentials and
history there.

Use staqly when content is produced faster than it is published: an agent drafts a week of
posts overnight, you look them over in the calendar, and a schedule sends one out each day to
the channels you chose.

## What it does

- **Holds the posts.** Text, media and per-channel variants, grouped in collections, each with
  a status (`draft`, `scheduled`, `publishing`, `published` or `failed`) and a full history.
- **Publishes them.** A post has one **target** per channel. At its time a durable job queue
  publishes every target, retries failures and records the link to the published post.
- **Keeps a rhythm.** A collection's schedules run once or on a rule (weekday mornings, every
  few hours) and publish the next waiting post, chosen posts or everything that waits. When
  nothing waits, a schedule can ask your agent for new posts or call an endpoint of yours.
- **Tells you and your tools.** Alerts on Telegram, Slack, Discord, ntfy or email say when a
  post went out or failed and when a channel needs reconnecting; signed webhooks deliver every
  event to your own services.

Everything runs on your machine: one binary (`staqly serve`, or the same app as one Docker
container), one SQLite file, one uploads folder, credentials sealed with a key that never
leaves it. No cloud account, no Redis, no Postgres.

## Two ways to run it

Staqly runs in one of two modes. Most people want **local mode**, the default.

| Aspect | **Local mode** (default) | **Cloud mode** |
|---|---|---|
| Who | You, on your own computer | A hosted, multi-tenant instance |
| Credentials | None: only this machine can connect | Scoped API keys `stk_...`, browser sessions, recovery keys |
| Workspaces | One, created automatically | Many: anyone (or only `SETUP_TOKEN` holders) can create one |
| OAuth platforms | You register a developer app per platform once | The operator's apps, or your own |
| Limits | Unlocked | Free or Pro ($30/mo, 7-day trial) |

Read [Local vs cloud](/local-vs-cloud) for the details.
