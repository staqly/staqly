This policy explains how **staqly, Switzerland** ("staqly", "we", "us") processes
personal data when you use **staqly cloud** (the hosted dashboard, API and MCP endpoint), the website
staqly.ai and this documentation site. We follow the Swiss Federal Act on Data Protection (FADP) and, for people in
the EU/EEA, the GDPR.

## Staqly on your own computer

If you run staqly yourself (`staqly serve` or Docker), your data stays on your machine. The
software has **no telemetry** and sends nothing to us. Outgoing requests happen only to
services you configure yourself: the platforms you connect as channels (your posts and media
go to them when published), webhooks, alerts (Telegram, Slack, Discord, ntfy, email) and the
endpoints your schedules call. If you set `POSTHOG_KEY` yourself, usage events and error
reports go to **your own** PostHog project ([Analytics](/self-hosting/analytics)), never to us.
The rest of this policy is about staqly cloud.

## Who is responsible

Staqly is the controller for account and usage data (section 1 below). For the content
you and your agents store in a workspace (section 2), **you** decide what goes in and why; we
process it on your behalf to provide the service.

Contact for privacy questions and requests: [support@staqly.ai](mailto:support@staqly.ai).

#### Free trial checks

The Starter plan has a free trial. So that it is offered once, staqly cloud keeps fingerprints of the accounts and
cards used during a trial:

- **What is stored.** A fingerprint is a keyed hash (HMAC, with a secret key only our servers hold) of a platform
  account id, of the platform id of the person who connected a shared space, or of Stripe's fingerprint of a card.
  It cannot be turned back into the id or the card; we can only check whether an id or card we are given matches
  one we have seen. We never store the ids, handles, card numbers or Stripe's card fingerprint themselves. Shared
  spaces such as a company's Slack workspace or a Discord server are never fingerprinted as such, so colleagues
  using the same space are not mistaken for one another. Spaces connected without a person (webhooks, bots,
  Telegram) have no fingerprint.
- **When it is used.** Only when a workspace on a free trial connects an account or adds a card that a free trial
  which ended without a payment, in the last 180 days, also used. A card match means no free trial: the plan is
  charged from the first day, and if the trial had already started, it ends early and the plan is charged. An
  account match never charges anyone: the trial is paused (no publishing and no new channels; your drafts and
  settings stay) and you choose whether to start the paid plan.
- **Your say.** These checks run automatically. If one pauses or ends your trial and you think it is wrong, write to
  [support@staqly.ai](mailto:support@staqly.ai): a person reviews it and can lift it. You can also object to this
  processing; we then weigh your reasons against preventing abuse of the trial.

We rely on our legitimate interest in preventing abuse of the free trial, which we have weighed against your
interests: the data is pseudonymised, used for this purpose only, kept briefly (see
[How long we keep data](#how-long-we-keep-data)) and never shared or used for anything else.

### 2. Content in your workspace

Collections, posts (text, media, per-channel texts and settings), metadata, media files, the
connected channels with their sealed credentials, schedules, activity history, webhook and
alert settings. We process it
only to provide staqly to you: store it, show it, search it, and send it where you configured
(the platforms you publish to, webhooks, alerts). We do not use it to train AI models, sell it, or show it to
other customers.

staqly also stores the public comments people leave on your published posts (their name or handle, text and
time) for 90 days by default, to show and summarise them. Self-hosted installs keep them on their own server.

Content may contain personal data about other people, for example in generated posts or
images. You are responsible for having a legal basis for putting it into staqly.

### 3. staqly.ai and this documentation site

staqly.ai and docs.staqly.ai are static sites; docs.staqly.ai is hosted on GitHub Pages, which
processes connection data (such as IP addresses) to deliver the pages; see
[GitHub's privacy statement](https://docs.github.com/en/site-policy/privacy-policies/github-general-privacy-statement).

To learn which pages people read and how they find us, both sites send usage events to
**PostHog** (hosted in the EU): the pages you visit, links and buttons you click, the referring
site and campaign tags, page load times, and your browser, device type and approximate location
derived from your IP address. They set **no cookies** and store nothing in your browser. Visits
are counted with an anonymous identifier that PostHog computes from a hash of the day, your IP
address, user agent and the site, with a secret that changes daily, so the same visitor cannot be
recognised on another day or across the two sites. We do not record sessions or what you type.
Legal basis: legitimate interest in improving the sites. You can block these events with any
content blocker.
The API playground keeps the API key you enter in your browser only and sends requests directly
from your browser to the instance you choose.

### 4. Product analytics and error reports in staqly cloud

To see which features are used, where onboarding stalls, why publishing fails and what breaks,
staqly cloud sends **PostHog** (hosted in the EU) a limited set of usage events and error
reports:

- **Usage events** from our server, after something happened: for example that you signed in
  (and with which method), created a workspace, connected a channel (which platform), created,
  scheduled or published a post (to which platforms, how many channels and media files, how
  late it went out), that publishing failed (the platform's error message), that you uploaded or
  edited media, started a checkout, or that your plan changed. They are linked to your account
  only by its internal id (`usr_...`) and to your workspace by its id; **not** your name, email
  address, IP address, post text, media, channel names, comments or any other content. Work we
  do for a workspace (such as publishing at the scheduled time) is recorded for the workspace,
  not for a person. At most once a day we record that you used the workspace.
- **Error reports** when something goes wrong: from our server, the error message and technical
  context (request ID, route, workspace ID); from the dashboard in your browser, the error, the
  page it happened on (without query strings) and your browser type.
- **Page views and load times** of the dashboard, **without cookies** or anything stored in
  your browser and not linked to your account: counted the same anonymous way as on our
  websites (section 3).

We do **not** record sessions, clicks or what you type, and never send access tokens, API keys,
passwords, cookies or payment details. Revenue figures come from Stripe, not from these events.
Legal basis: legitimate interest in running a reliable service and improving it. You can block
the dashboard's events with any content blocker; to object to the server's events, write to
[support@staqly.ai](mailto:support@staqly.ai).

## Cookies

The dashboard uses only what it needs to work: an HttpOnly **session cookie** (valid for up to
30 days) and a CSRF token, plus a few display preferences (such as the theme) in your browser's local storage. No advertising or tracking
cookies.

## Who else processes data

- **Hosting and infrastructure:** the providers that store and run staqly cloud for us, and the
  email provider that delivers sign-in codes and invitations, under data processing agreements. Ask us at support@staqly.ai for the current list.
- **Analytics and error reports:** PostHog (EU cloud), for staqly.ai and docs.staqly.ai
  (section 3) and staqly cloud (section 4); the software you run yourself sends us nothing.
- **Payments:** Stripe, once payments are available. Stripe receives your card details
  directly; we never see them.
- **Services you connect:** when you connect a channel, set up webhooks, alerts or schedule endpoints,
  staqly sends data to (or fetches from) those services on your instruction. Their own terms and
  policies apply. When you use the AI tools (the design editor's, the media library's AI videos), your
  description and the picture you change go to fal.ai or, when fal.ai is not available, Higgsfield, on the server
  operator's account; staqly keeps them for a day at most unless you keep the result. A server whose operator added a Pixabay key sends your stock photo searches to
  Pixabay; your browser loads the thumbnails from it, and a photo you add is copied into your library.
  When you use the assistant or write a post with AI, your messages, the files you attach and what the
  assistant reads from your workspace to answer go to the language model the server operator set up
  (through fal.ai, or another provider of their choice); staqly keeps your chats with the assistant until you
  delete them.

We do not sell personal data. We disclose it to authorities only when the law requires it.

## International transfers

When data is processed outside Switzerland and the EU/EEA, we rely on adequacy decisions or
on standard contractual clauses recognised under the FADP and GDPR.

## How long we keep data

- **Workspace content** stays until you delete it. Deleting a post, purging it, or deleting
  the whole workspace removes it from the live system right away; it may remain in our backups
  until they are rotated out, which happens automatically on a regular schedule.
- **Workspace exports** (ZIP downloads) are deleted after 24 hours.
- **Sessions** expire after 30 days or when you sign out or revoke them.
- **Request logs** are kept only as long as needed for security and troubleshooting, then
  deleted.
- **Analytics events and error reports** (sections 3 and 4) are kept in PostHog for at most
  the retention of our PostHog plan, currently 12 months.
- **Billing records** are kept as long as accounting law requires (in Switzerland, 10 years).
- **Free trial fingerprints** ([Free trial checks](#free-trial-checks)) are deleted within a day once the workspace
  pays, and with the workspace when it is deleted. After a free trial that ended without a payment they are kept for
  180 days, also when that workspace is deleted, and then deleted.

## Security

All traffic is encrypted with HTTPS. API keys, sessions, recovery keys and sign-in links are
stored only as hashes; channel credentials and alert secrets are encrypted at rest. Workspaces are
isolated from each other in every query. See [Security](/self-hosting/security) for details.

## Data from Facebook, Instagram and Threads

When you connect a Facebook Page, an Instagram account or a Threads account, staqly keeps what it needs to
publish for you: the access token Meta issues (encrypted at rest), the account or Page ID, its name and
profile picture, and the IDs of the posts it publishes and the comments on them. staqly uses them only to
publish, schedule and show your posts and their comments; it does not sell them or use them for advertising.

To delete this data:

- **In staqly:** open **Channels**, choose the channel and **Remove** it. Its token and account details are
  deleted from the live system right away (backups as described in [How long we keep data](#how-long-we-keep-data)).
  Deleting the workspace removes all its channels.
- **On Meta:** remove staqly from the apps that may use your account: on Facebook **Settings & privacy →
  Settings → Apps and websites**, on Instagram **Settings → Website permissions → Apps and websites**, on Threads
  **Settings → Account → Website permissions**. staqly can then no longer use the token; remove the channel in
  staqly as well to delete what it stored.
- **By email:** write to [support@staqly.ai](mailto:support@staqly.ai) and we delete it for you within 30 days.

## Your rights

You can ask for access to your personal data, a copy in a portable format, correction,
deletion, restriction, or object to processing based on legitimate interest. Much of this you
can do yourself:

- **Export** your data on every plan: **Settings → Data & privacy → Export workspace** (or
  [the API](/self-hosting/data-export)). On Starter the export does not include uploaded files;
  on Plus and above it does.
- **Delete** posts, or the whole workspace: **Settings → Data & privacy → Delete workspace**.

For anything else, write to [support@staqly.ai](mailto:support@staqly.ai). We answer within 30
days. You can also complain to a supervisory authority: in Switzerland the Federal Data
Protection and Information Commissioner (FDPIC), or the authority in your EU/EEA country.
