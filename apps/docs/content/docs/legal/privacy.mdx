This policy explains how **staqly, Switzerland** ("staqly", "we", "us") processes
personal data when you use **staqly cloud** (the hosted dashboard, API and MCP endpoint) and this
documentation site. We follow the Swiss Federal Act on Data Protection (FADP) and, for people in
the EU/EEA, the GDPR.

## staqly on your own computer

If you run staqly yourself (`staqly serve` or Docker), your data stays on your machine. The
software has **no telemetry** and sends nothing to us. Outgoing requests happen only to
services you configure yourself: webhooks, notification channels (Telegram, Slack, Discord,
ntfy, email) and watcher sources. Item summaries and tags are written by your own AI: with
`staqly serve`, the Claude Code or Codex CLI installed on your computer (so the item text goes to
that provider under your own account), or the model endpoint you set in `TAGGER_URL`. Set
`TAGGER_COMMAND=off` to turn this off. The rest of this policy is about staqly cloud.

## Who is responsible

staqly is the controller for account and usage data (section 1 below). For the content
you and your agents store in a workspace (section 2), **you** decide what goes in and why; we
process it on your behalf to provide the service.

Contact for privacy questions and requests: [support@staqly.ai](mailto:support@staqly.ai).

## What we process and why

### 1. Account and usage data

| Data | Why | Legal basis |
|---|---|---|
| Workspace name, plan, trial and billing period, usage counters | Run your workspace, enforce plan limits, the Pro trial | Contract |
| Hashed API keys, recovery key and session tokens (never stored in plain text) | Sign you in and keep the workspace secure | Contract |
| Browser sessions: user agent, sign-in time, last use | Show your signed-in devices so you can revoke them | Contract, security |
| Request logs: IP address, time, path, status, user agent, request ID | Security, abuse prevention, rate limits, troubleshooting | Legitimate interest |
| Billing data (once payments are available): name, email, country, invoices | Charge for Pro and keep accounting records | Contract, legal obligation |
| Messages you send us | Answer you | Contract, legitimate interest |

### 2. Content in your workspace

Collections, items (text, images, video, links, JSON), metadata, media files, decisions and
feedback, activity history, watchers, webhook and notification settings. We process it
only to provide staqly to you: store it, show it, search it, and send it where you configured
(webhooks, notification channels). We do not use it to train AI models, sell it, or show it to
other customers.

Content may contain personal data about other people, for example in generated posts or
images. You are responsible for having a legal basis for putting it into staqly.

### 3. This documentation site

docs.staqly.ai is a static site hosted on GitHub Pages. It sets no cookies and uses no
analytics. GitHub processes connection data (such as IP addresses) to deliver the pages; see
[GitHub's privacy statement](https://docs.github.com/en/site-policy/privacy-policies/github-general-privacy-statement).
The API playground keeps the API key you enter in your browser only and sends requests directly
from your browser to the instance you choose.

## Cookies

The dashboard uses only what it needs to work: an HttpOnly **session cookie** (valid for up to
30 days) and a CSRF token, plus your theme choice in local storage. No advertising or tracking
cookies.

## Who else processes data

- **Hosting and infrastructure:** the providers that store and run staqly cloud for us, under
  data processing agreements. Ask us at support@staqly.ai for the current list.
- **Payments:** Stripe, once payments are available. Stripe receives your card details
  directly; we never see them.
- **Services you connect:** when you set up webhooks, notification channels or watchers, staqly
  sends data to (or fetches from) those services on your instruction. Their own terms and
  policies apply.

We do not sell personal data. We disclose it to authorities only when the law requires it.

## International transfers

When data is processed outside Switzerland and the EU/EEA, we rely on adequacy decisions or
on standard contractual clauses recognised under the FADP and GDPR.

## How long we keep data

- **Workspace content** stays until you delete it. Deleting an item, purging it, or deleting
  the whole workspace removes it from the live system right away; it may remain in our backups
  until they are rotated out, which happens automatically on a regular schedule.
- **Workspace exports** (ZIP downloads) are deleted after 24 hours.
- **Sessions** expire after 30 days or when you sign out or revoke them.
- **Request logs** are kept only as long as needed for security and troubleshooting, then
  deleted.
- **Billing records** are kept as long as accounting law requires (in Switzerland, 10 years).

## Security

All traffic is encrypted with HTTPS. API keys, sessions, recovery keys and sign-in links are
stored only as hashes; secrets for notification channels are encrypted at rest. Workspaces are
isolated from each other in every query. See [Security](/self-hosting/security) for details.

## Your rights

You can ask for access to your personal data, a copy in a portable format, correction,
deletion, restriction, or object to processing based on legitimate interest. Much of this you
can do yourself:

- **Export** everything: **Settings → Data & privacy → Export workspace** (or
  [the API](/self-hosting/data-export)).
- **Delete** items, or the whole workspace: **Settings → Data & privacy → Delete workspace**.

For anything else, write to [support@staqly.ai](mailto:support@staqly.ai). We answer within 30
days. You can also complain to a supervisory authority: in Switzerland the Federal Data
Protection and Information Commissioner (FDPIC), or the authority in your EU/EEA country.

## Changes

We may update this policy; the date at the top shows the latest version. We will notify
workspace owners in advance of material changes.
