Plans set how much each workspace may use on [Staqly Cloud](/local-vs-cloud#staqly-cloud): channels, storage, posts
to X and so on. Prices are in euros, **exclude VAT** (added at checkout where it applies) and are
**per user**: every member of the workspace is a seat billed at the plan's price,
whatever their role. Posts, collections and schedules are unlimited on every plan. If you run
**Staqly Community Edition** on your own computer or server (`staqly serve` or the Docker image),
plans don't apply to you: its plan is **Community Edition**, with no limits (usage is still shown
for information).

| Posts per month | Unlimited | Unlimited | Unlimited | Unlimited |
| Collections and schedules | Unlimited | Unlimited | Unlimited | Unlimited |
| Connected channels | 5 | 15 | 40 | Unlimited |
| Posts to X per month | 250 | 450 | 950 | 1,600 |
| Posts to LinkedIn per month | 300 | 600 | 1,200 | 2,500 |
| LinkedIn posts with a video per month | 30 | 60 | 150 | 300 |
| People | Per user | Per user | Per user | Per user |
| Alerts | 3 | 10 | Unlimited | Unlimited |
| Webhooks | 3 | 10 | Unlimited | Unlimited |
| Active API keys | 2 | 10 | Unlimited | Unlimited |
| Storage | 10 GB | 50 GB | 200 GB | 1 TB |
| Max file size | 25 MB | 100 MB | 250 MB | 1 GB |
| Workspace data export (ZIP of collections, posts, channels, schedules, events) | Yes, without files | Yes, with files | Yes, with files | Yes, with files |
| Backups and activity log export | No | Yes | Yes | Yes |
| Priority support | No | No | No | Yes |

Signed webhooks, MCP, the REST API, the SDKs and the full history are on every plan. Every
plan can [export its data](/self-hosting/data-export) (**Settings → Data & privacy**): on
Starter the workspace ZIP is data only (`includes_media: false`), with media files listed by id,
name and type but not included. The ZIP with the media files, backups (**Settings → Backups**)
and the activity log export come with Plus and above: on Starter
`GET /v1/events/export` answers `402 plan_limit_exceeded` (limit `data_export`). The Community Edition
always has everything.

The compare page on staqly.ai lists the same limits side by side.

## The Starter trial

1. A new workspace has no plan yet and doesn't open until it has one: the last welcome step offers
   **Starter free for 7 days**. The trial is Stripe's: you add a card at checkout, nothing is
   charged until it ends, and you can cancel anytime before. Only Starter has a trial; Plus, Pro and
   Business are paid from the first day.
2. Each workspace gets the trial **once**, and one workspace per owner at a time: a workspace you
   add while another of yours is still in its trial is charged on the day you choose its plan (the plan
   picker says so under the button, before Stripe opens). `GET /v1/billing` reports it as `trial`:
   `available`, `used`, `other_workspace` or `none`.
3. A workspace you add (**New workspace**) opens on the welcome steps, the plan last; cancelling Stripe's
   checkout brings you back to that step.
4. Choose a plan any time: **Settings → Plan & usage → Choose a plan**, the user menu, or
   `POST /v1/billing/checkout` with the plan and interval.
   - Leaving the trial for another plan ends the trial at once: the new plan is charged right away and
     starts when the payment goes through. If the card fails, nothing changes (the trial keeps running)
     and the plan page says so.
   - **Promotion codes:** Stripe Checkout has its own code box for a first plan. To switch plans with a
     code, or apply one to the plan you have, use **Have a promotion code?** on the plan page (or send
     `promotion_code` to `POST /v1/billing/checkout`). The code is checked with Stripe before it is applied
     (`POST /v1/billing/promotion-code`; codes are case sensitive: `SPRING20`, not `spring20`), and the plans it covers show their discounted price; a code that is
     unknown, used up or made for other plans is refused with the reason. One discount at a time: while
     the subscription has one, the plan page shows it with its end date instead of **Have a promotion code?**,
     and another code (or the same one again) is refused until it ends.
5. Until a plan starts, and after one ends (a trial cancelled for want of a card, an unpaid renewal,
   a cancellation), the workspace is **locked**:
   - the dashboard shows only that, with **Go to billing** (Settings → Plan & usage) for owners and
     admins; paying there opens it again as soon as Stripe confirms;
   - the API still answers reads (`GET` requests, read-only MCP tools, the workspace export);
   - every change (new posts, publishing, channels, schedules and so on) answers
     **402 `plan_expired`**, and schedules stop running;
   - your data stays; paying for a plan lifts the suspension when Stripe confirms.

The same applies when a paid period ends unpaid. `GET /v1/workspace/usage` shows
`plan_expires_at` (end of the trial or paid period, `null` = no end) and `suspended`.

There is no free plan. Workspaces created before the plans above were on a free plan; they are
now an ended Starter period (read-only) until a plan is paid for.

## How limits are counted

- Limits apply **per workspace**: every API key of a workspace shares its plan.
- **Seats** are the workspace's members, whatever their role, and each one is billed at the plan's
  price per user (a team of three on Plus pays 3 × €29 = €87 a month). An invitation adds a seat
  once it is accepted and the rest of the period is charged at once; removing someone (or someone
  leaving) takes it away and the unused rest is credited on the next invoice, so a person pays for
  exactly the days they were a member; during the Starter trial seats are free and the first charge uses the
  seats at the trial's end. No plan caps the seats. Agents and API keys are not seats and cost
  nothing.
- **Posts to X** are counted because X charges for every post sent through its API: each post and
  thread reply counts once when it goes out (a post with a live link counts 14; X channels remove
  or defang links by default). The plan's posts reset every month. **Extra posts** come in
  one-time bundles, **Settings → Plan & usage → Buy more X posts**: 200 for €10, 1,000 for €40 or
  4,000 for €150, excl. VAT. They are used after the included ones and expire at the end of the
  billing period. When both run out, publishing to X answers **402 `x_posts_exhausted`** (and a
  post scheduled for later fails with the reason). `GET /v1/billing/x-posts` shows what's left.
- **Posts to LinkedIn** are counted because LinkedIn limits how many posts and video uploads all
  of staqly's workspaces together can send each day, so each plan includes a fair share: every post
  to a LinkedIn profile or Page counts once when it goes out, and a post with a video also counts on
  the video allowance. Comments don't count. The allowances reset every month and can't be bought
  on top. When one is used up, publishing to LinkedIn answers **402 `linkedin_posts_exhausted`** or
  **`linkedin_videos_exhausted`** (and a post scheduled for later fails with the reason).
  `GET /v1/workspace/usage` shows both counts. Other platforms are not counted.
- Channels, alerts, webhooks, API keys and storage are counted from what currently exists; a
  disabled channel still counts.
- **Posts / month** is unlimited on every plan; the count (every post created per calendar month,
  UTC, by people, agents, duplicates or schedules) is still shown in usage.

## Checking usage

- Dashboard: **Settings → Plan & usage** ([details](/dashboard/plan-and-usage)): the plan, the
  usage, add-ons, the card and the invoices.
- API: `GET /v1/plans` lists the plans with `price_month`, `price_year` (per user, in `currency`: `eur`) and their limits
  (`-1` = unlimited); `GET /v1/workspace/usage` returns the current plan, the period (`2026-10`)
  and usage (including `seats`), plus `posts_by_status` and `webhook_deliveries`.

## What happens when you hit a limit

A request over a limit fails with **HTTP 402** and machine-readable `details`:

```json
{
  "error": {
    "code": "plan_limit_exceeded",
    "message": "The Starter plan allows 5 channels; upgrade the workspace plan to continue.",
    "details": { "limit": "channels", "max": 5, "used": 5, "plan": "starter" }
  }
}
```

`details.limit` is one of `posts_per_month`, `channels`, `alerts`, `webhooks`,
`api_keys`, `storage_bytes`, `max_upload_bytes` or `data_export`.

## Changing a workspace's plan

A workspace changes plan when its account owner pays with a card: the plan picker (**Settings → Plan & usage → Choose a plan** or **Change plan**, or the user menu)
has a **Monthly / Yearly** switch and one card per plan. Yearly prices are twelve whole-dollar
months at 20 % off, rounded down, so the saving is 20 % or a little more.

- Without a subscription, a card opens Stripe Checkout (`POST /v1/billing/checkout` with
  `{"plan":"plus","interval":"year"}`, account owner). When Stripe confirms the payment, the
  workspace moves to that plan until the end of the paid period plus 3 days of grace.
- With a subscription, a card switches it to that plan or interval at once; Stripe charges or
  credits the difference (proration). The plan changes when Stripe's event arrives.
- **Manage subscription** opens the Stripe customer portal (`POST /v1/billing/portal`) to change
  the card, download invoices or cancel. When a subscription is canceled or stays unpaid, the
  plan ends and the workspace is suspended until it pays again. `GET /v1/billing` shows the
  subscription status.

Every plan change is recorded as a `workspace.plan_changed` [event](/api/events#other-events)
with the reason (for example `stripe:active`).

The plan table lives in `apps/api/internal/app/plans.go`, the single source of truth for
limits and for the pricing shown in the dashboard.
