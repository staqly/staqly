Plans set how much each workspace may use on [Staqly Cloud](/local-vs-cloud#staqly-cloud): channels, storage, posts
to X and so on. Prices are in euros, **exclude VAT** (added at checkout where it applies) and are
**per user**: every member of the workspace is a seat billed at the plan's price,
whatever their role. All usage limits are shared by the workspace and do not multiply with seats. Collections and schedules have no count limit; created posts do. If you run
**Staqly Community Edition** on your own computer or server (`staqly serve` or the Docker image),
plans don't apply to you: its plan is **Community Edition**, with no limits (usage is still shown
for information).

Signed webhooks, MCP, the REST API, the SDKs and the full history are on every plan, and so are the AI tools (the
[assistant](/dashboard/assistant) and the [design editor's AI](/dashboard/design-editor#ai-tools)): the assistant, image and video generation share the monthly AI allowance above. New requests reserve their
estimated cost before running and stop with `402 ai_limit_reached` if there is not enough left. The allowance
renews on the 1st in UTC, does not roll over, and does not trigger automatic overage charges. Manual editing
continues to work. Every
plan can [export its data](/self-hosting/data-export) (**Settings → Data & privacy**): on
Starter the workspace ZIP is data only (`includes_media: false`), with media files listed by id,
name and type but not included. The ZIP with the media files, backups (**Settings → Backups**)
and the activity log export come with Plus and above: on Starter
`GET /v1/events/export` answers `402 plan_limit_exceeded` (limit `data_export`). The Community Edition
always has everything.

The compare page on staqly.ai lists the same limits side by side.

## The Starter trial

1. A new workspace has no plan yet and doesn't open until it has one: the last welcome step offers
   **Starter free for 7 days**. The trial is Stripe's: you add a card at checkout, nothing is
   charged until it ends, and you can cancel anytime before. Only Starter has a trial; Plus, Pro and
   Business are paid from the first day.
2. Each workspace gets the trial **once**, and one workspace per owner at a time: a workspace you
   add while another of yours is still in its trial is charged on the day you choose its plan (the plan
   picker says so under the button, before Stripe opens). `GET /v1/billing` reports it as `trial`:
   `available`, `used`, `other_workspace` or `none`.
3. A workspace you add (**New workspace**) opens on the welcome steps, the plan last; cancelling Stripe's
   checkout brings you back to that step.
4. **One free trial per card and per person.** A trial that ends without ever being paid for
   (cancelled, or the first charge never went through) is remembered for 180 days by the card it was
   started with and by who its channels belong to.
   - **The card.** A card that such a trial used gets no new trial: when it is already on file, the
     plan picker says *No free trial: this card was already used for one* before Stripe opens, and
     Checkout charges the plan at once. When the card only shows up in Checkout, the trial ends as soon
     as Stripe reports it and the card is charged then; the dashboard says why (*No free trial for
     this card*), and `GET /v1/billing` reports `trial_revoked: "card"`.
   - **The accounts.** Connecting channels never charges anyone. A personal account (an X or Bluesky
     account, a LinkedIn member, a YouTube channel...) counts as itself. A space many people share
     (a Slack workspace, a Facebook or LinkedIn Page, a Google Business location) counts as the person
     who connected it, never the space, so colleagues are never taken for one another; a space connected
     with no person behind it (a Discord or Telegram bot, a webhook) doesn't count. When a workspace in
     its trial connects (or reconnects) one personal account of such a trial, or the people behind two of
     its shared spaces, its trial is **paused**: posts aren't published and new channels can't be
     connected (**402 `trial_already_used`**), while drafts and settings stay. The dashboard explains it
     with **Start paid plan now** (the trial ends and the card is charged:
     `POST /v1/billing/start-paid-plan`) and **This isn't right? Contact us**; `GET /v1/billing` reports
     `trial_review: "channels"`.
   - Removing and adding an account again in the same workspace, brand-new accounts, a workspace
     that paid, and a trial still running in another workspace are never affected. Once a workspace
     has paid, its accounts and card no longer count against anyone. Staqly support can lift a pause
     that was a mistake.
   - What is kept: a keyed hash (HMAC with the server's secret key) of each personal account's platform
     id (or its handle where the platform gives no id), of the platform id of the person who connected
     a shared space, and of Stripe's card fingerprint, each with its kind, the workspace and when its
     trial ended unpaid. Never the ids, the handles or anything about the card itself. Installs without
     billing, and local mode, keep none of it.
5. Choose a plan any time: **Settings → Plan & usage → Choose a plan**, the user menu, or
   `POST /v1/billing/checkout` with the plan and interval.
   - Leaving the trial for another plan ends the trial at once: the new plan is charged right away and
     starts when the payment goes through. If it doesn't, nothing changes (the trial keeps running) and
     the dashboard opens a payment page that says why, with the next step on Stripe's own pages:
     - **Your bank asks you to confirm the payment** (3-D Secure, common for European cards): **Confirm
       the payment** opens Stripe's invoice page in a new tab, where you confirm it with your bank and pay.
       Come back to the dashboard tab and the new plan shows once Stripe has the payment.
     - **The card was declined** (for example not enough funds, or an expired card): **Update your card**
       opens the billing portal; then choose the plan again. You can also pay on Stripe's invoice page with
       another card.

     Card details and confirmations never go through staqly. Over the API, `POST /v1/billing/checkout`
     answers `402 payment_failed` with `details.reason` (`authentication_required`, `card_declined`,
     `insufficient_funds`, ...) and `details.pay_url` (Stripe's invoice page), and
     `GET /v1/billing/pending-payment` reads the same later.
   - **Promotion codes:** Stripe Checkout has its own code box for a first plan. To switch plans with a
     code, or apply one to the plan you have, use **Have a promotion code?** on the plan page (or send
     `promotion_code` to `POST /v1/billing/checkout`). The code is checked with Stripe before it is applied
     (`POST /v1/billing/promotion-code`; codes are case sensitive: `SPRING20`, not `spring20`), and the plans it covers show their discounted price; a code that is
     unknown, used up or made for other plans is refused with the reason. One discount at a time: while
     the subscription has one, the plan page shows it with its end date instead of **Have a promotion code?**,
     and another code (or the same one again) is refused until it ends.
6. Until a plan starts, and after one ends (a trial cancelled for want of a card, an unpaid renewal,
   a cancellation, a trial ended for its card whose charge didn't go through), the workspace is **locked**:
   - the dashboard shows only that, with **Go to billing** (Settings → Plan & usage) for owners and
     admins; paying there opens it again as soon as Stripe confirms;
   - the API still answers reads (`GET` requests, read-only MCP tools, the workspace export);
   - every change (new posts, publishing, channels, schedules and so on) answers
     **402 `plan_expired`**, and schedules stop running;
   - your data stays; paying for a plan lifts the suspension when Stripe confirms.

The same applies when a paid period ends unpaid. `GET /v1/workspace/usage` shows
`plan_expires_at` (end of the trial or paid period, `null` = no end) and `suspended`.

There is no free plan. Workspaces created before the plans above were on a free plan; they are
now an ended Starter period (read-only) until a plan is paid for.

## How limits are counted

- Limits apply **per workspace**: every API key of a workspace shares its plan.
- **Seats** are the workspace's members, whatever their role, and each one is billed at the plan's
  price per user (a team of three on Plus pays 3 × €29 = €87 a month). An invitation adds a seat
  once it is accepted and the rest of the period is charged at once; removing someone (or someone
  leaving) takes it away and the unused rest is credited on the next invoice, so a person pays for
  exactly the days they were a member; during the Starter trial seats are free and the first charge uses the
  seats at the trial's end. No plan caps the seats. Agents and API keys are not seats and cost
  nothing.
- **Posts to X** are counted because X charges for every post sent through its API: each post and
  thread reply needs one unit (a post with a live link needs 14; X channels remove
  or defang links by default). The plan's posts reset every month. **Extra posts** come in
  one-time bundles, **Settings → Plan & usage → Buy more** (next to X): 200 for €10, 1,000 for €40 or
  4,000 for €150, excl. VAT. They are used after the included ones and expire at the end of the
  billing period. When both run out, publishing to X answers **402 `x_posts_exhausted`** (and a
  post scheduled for later fails with the reason). `GET /v1/billing/x-posts` shows what's left.
- **Posts to LinkedIn** are counted because LinkedIn limits how many posts and video uploads all
  of staqly's workspaces together can send each day, so each plan includes a fair share: every post
  to a LinkedIn profile or Page needs one unit, and a post with a video also counts on
  the video allowance. Comments don't count. The allowances reset every month and can't be bought
  on top. When one is used up, publishing to LinkedIn answers **402 `linkedin_posts_exhausted`** or
  **`linkedin_videos_exhausted`** (and a post scheduled for later fails with the reason).
  `GET /v1/workspace/usage` shows both counts. Daily shared-capacity safeguards also apply; see below.
- Channels, alerts, webhooks, API keys and storage are counted from what currently exists; a
  disabled channel still counts.
- **New posts / month** counts every created post, including drafts, duplicates and posts made by agents or
  schedules, per calendar month in UTC. Editing an existing post does not count again; deleting one does not
  refund its allowance. Publishing one post to several channels counts once here, but each X or LinkedIn
  target consumes that platform's allowance. Thread replies consume X credits separately.

X and LinkedIn allowances are reserved when a worker starts publishing, so concurrent posts cannot
spend the same remaining allowance. Usage includes these in-progress reservations. A terminal rejection or an explicit capacity deferral
returns a fresh reservation. Other retryable failures and uncertain outcomes (such as a lost response)
keep it reserved; the post may already be live. Retrying that target uses its original reservation, including across a month boundary.
Changing a retry to need more units requires restoring its original content or creating a new post.
Scheduled posts cancelled before publishing do not reserve an allowance.

## Shared platform capacity

Monthly allowances are not a guarantee that everything can be sent on the same day. Cloud publishing,
replies, reposts and statistics refreshes also share the operator's daily API capacity:

- LinkedIn: by default, 400 API calls per day for the service, at most 40 per workspace. Video preparation and
  polling count too; 40 calls is not 40 guaranteed posts. The counter resets at midnight UTC.
- YouTube: by default, 90 upload starts per day for the service, at most 9 per workspace. It resets at midnight
  Pacific Time, including daylight-saving changes. These are upload attempts, not successful videos.
- X statistics: 100 / 200 / 500 / 1,000 fresh single-post reads per workspace per UTC calendar month.
  Cached Analytics views do not consume reads. Extra X publishing bundles do not increase this allowance.

No workspace may exceed its own daily cap; this is not a guaranteed reservation, and the shared pool can fill first. Failed calls and retries still consume daily
capacity. Jobs report the reason when capacity is unavailable; monthly allowances never override provider
restrictions. The operator must check the actual approved quotas and leave room for account connections,
token refreshes and other activity. See [configuration](/self-hosting/configuration#shared-publishing-capacity).
Local installs have no staqly capacity caps, but their providers' limits and bills still apply.

## Checking usage

- Dashboard: **Settings → Plan & usage** ([details](/dashboard/plan-and-usage)): the plan, the
  usage, add-ons, the card and the invoices.
- API: `GET /v1/plans` lists the plans with `price_month`, `price_year` (per user, in `currency`: `eur`) and their limits
  (`-1` = unlimited); `GET /v1/workspace/usage` returns the current plan, the period (`2026-10`)
  and usage (including `seats`), plus `posts_by_status` and `webhook_deliveries`.

## What happens when you hit a limit

A request over a limit fails with **HTTP 402** and machine-readable `details`:

```json
{
  "error": {
    "code": "plan_limit_exceeded",
    "message": "The Starter plan allows 5 channels; upgrade the workspace plan to continue.",
    "details": { "limit": "channels", "max": 5, "used": 5, "plan": "starter" }
  }
}
```

`details.limit` is one of `posts_per_month`, `channels`, `alerts`, `webhooks`,
`api_keys`, `storage_bytes`, `max_upload_bytes` or `data_export`.

## Changing a workspace's plan

A workspace changes plan when its account owner pays with a card: the plan picker (**Settings → Plan & usage → Choose a plan** or **Change plan**, or the user menu)
has a **Monthly / Yearly** switch and one card per plan. Yearly prices are twelve whole-euro
months at 20 % off, rounded down, so the saving is 20 % or a little more.

- Without a subscription, a card opens Stripe Checkout (`POST /v1/billing/checkout` with
  `{"plan":"plus","interval":"year"}`, account owner). When Stripe confirms the payment, the
  workspace moves to that plan until the end of the paid period plus 3 days of grace.
- With a subscription, a card switches it to that plan or interval at once; Stripe charges or
  credits the difference (proration). The plan changes when Stripe's event arrives.
- **Manage subscription** opens the Stripe customer portal (`POST /v1/billing/portal`) to change
  the card, download invoices or cancel. When a subscription is canceled or stays unpaid, the
  plan ends and the workspace is suspended until it pays again. `GET /v1/billing` shows the
  subscription status.

Every plan change is recorded as a `workspace.plan_changed` [event](/api/events#other-events)
with the reason (for example `stripe:active`).

The plan table lives in `apps/api/internal/app/plans.go`, the single source of truth for
limits and for the pricing shown in the dashboard.
