---
title: Share a preview
description: Send a link that shows a post as each channel will, and collect comments from people who don't use Staqly.
---

Anyone with a preview link sees the post as each of its channels will show it and can leave a
comment there. They don't need an account or to sign in. Use it to get a client's or a
teammate's view before a post goes out, or to show what went out.

## Make a link

1. Open the post and click the **eye** icon (**Preview and feedback**).
2. Pick when the link expires (**Never**, **In 1 day**, **In 7 days**, **In 30 days**) and click
   **Create link**.
3. Copy the link and send it. It is shown only now; make a new one if you lose it.

**Revoke** next to a link stops it at once. Every post can have several links, each with its own
expiry, so you can stop one person's link without the others.

Over the API: `POST /v1/posts/{id}/previews` with `expires_in_days` (0 to 365, 0 never) answers
the `url` once; `GET .../previews` lists them without their tokens and
`DELETE .../previews/{preview}` revokes one. Agents use the MCP tools `create_preview_link`,
`list_preview_links` and `revoke_preview_link`, and the CLI has `staqly posts share <id>`.

## What the preview shows

- Each channel's version of the post: the account's picture, name and handle, the text that
  channel gets (its own version if you wrote one, links handled as the channel does), the
  pictures and videos, and the comments that go out under it.
- Whether the post is a draft, scheduled (with its time) or published, and a link to it on each
  platform once it is out.
- The comment threads so far, with names but never emails.

The link's token sits after `#` in the address, so it doesn't reach server logs or other sites.

## Comments

Reviewers can comment on the whole post or on a passage:

- **On a passage:** select words in a channel's version (the post's text or one of the comments
  under it) and choose **Comment** next to the selection, or press **Ctrl+Alt+M**
  (**Cmd+Option+M** on a Mac). The words stay marked in the post; clicking them opens their
  comment, and hovering a comment marks its words.
- **General:** write in the comment box without selecting anything.

The first time, the page asks for a name (an email is optional and only your team sees it). It
remembers both in that browser.

Comments are threads, like in a shared document: anyone with the link can **Reply**, **Resolve**
a thread or **Reopen** it. Resolved threads fold into **Resolved** and their words are no longer
marked. If the post's text changes, a marked passage follows its words; when the words are gone,
the comment shows **Text changed** with the words it was about. The page refreshes every 30
seconds, so several reviewers see each other's comments.

Your team sees the same threads under **Preview and feedback** on the post (the eye icon counts
the open ones), replies as itself, resolves and removes comments (removing a thread's first
comment removes its replies). New reviewer comments also show up in the notifications bell, in
Activity as **Feedback on a preview**, and as the `post.feedback_added` event for webhooks.

A link takes up to 30 comments an hour, and one address up to 20 comments or changes every 10
minutes.

Over the API: `GET /v1/posts/{id}/comments` (with emails), `POST .../comments` (a reply with
`parent_id`, or a new thread with `quote`, `channel_id`, `part` and `anchor_start`/`anchor_end`),
`POST .../comments/{comment}/resolve` and `.../reopen`, `DELETE .../comments/{comment}`. Agents
use the MCP tools `list_post_comments`, `reply_to_post_comment` and `resolve_post_comment`; the
CLI has `staqly posts comments <id>`.

## Local installs

A local install serves the preview on its dashboard address. A phone or another computer opens
it only when it can reach that address on your network, like the dashboard itself.
