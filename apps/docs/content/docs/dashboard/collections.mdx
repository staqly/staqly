title: Collections
description: Group posts into collections, give each one a colour and a processing timeout, manage its schedules and archive or delete it.
---

A **collection** is a named group of posts with its own schedules, like a folder with a
calendar: *Product updates*, *Instagram Avatar*, *Weekly digest*. Use collections to keep
each project's or agent's posts together and to give them a rhythm. See
[Concepts](/concepts#collections) for how collections, posts and schedules fit together.

Collections live in the left pane of [Posts](/dashboard/posts): every collection with its
colour dot and post count, **New** at the top, **Show archived** at the bottom. Click one to see
its posts; **Settings** in the header opens its settings.

## Create a collection

<Steps>
<Step>

### Open the drawer

Click **New** at the top of the collections pane, or use the command palette
(<Kbd>⌘</Kbd> <Kbd>K</Kbd>). The form opens in a drawer: the header stays put, the fields
scroll, **Cancel** and **Create collection** sit at the bottom.

</Step>
<Step>

### Name it

Give it a **Name** (required, marked with `*`, up to 100 characters), an optional
**Description** (up to 2,000 characters), **Fail publishing if nothing is reported within**
(how long a post on [your own automation](/channels/your-own-automation) may wait for a result;
**Default** names the server's value) and a **Colour**. Names are unique within the workspace,
so agents can refer to a collection by name: `create_post` with
`collection: "Product updates"` just works. If a field is invalid, it turns red and the message
appears under it.

</Step>
<Step>

### Add schedules later

A new collection has no schedules; add them in its settings (see [below](#collection-settings)).

</Step>
</Steps>

Agents can create collections too. Ask *"Create a collection called Weekly digest"* and the
agent uses the `create_collection` MCP tool or `POST /v1/collections`.

### Colours

The colour shows as a dot in the collections pane and as the left bar of calendar chips. Pick
one of eight colours (green `#22c55e`, blue `#3b82f6`, violet `#8b5cf6`, pink `#ec4899`, red
`#ef4444`, orange `#f97316`, yellow `#eab308`, teal `#14b8a6`), *No colour*, or the rainbow
swatch, **Custom colour**: a picker with a saturation and brightness square, a hue slider, a hex
field such as `#1e3a8a` and a preview. Agents and scripts can set any hex colour through the API
(`"color": "#3b82f6"`).

## Collection settings

Click **Settings** in the collection's header. The drawer has three sections; one **Save
changes** in its footer saves them together:

| Section | What you can do |
|---|---|
| **General** | Change the name (required), description, **Fail publishing if nothing is reported within** (1 minute to 24 hours, or *Default*) and the colour. |
| **Schedules** (folded) | The collection's schedules, each with an on/off switch, **Run now**, **Edit** and **Delete**; **New schedule** opens the schedule drawer. See [Schedules](/automation/schedules). |
| **Danger zone** (folded) | **Archive collection** hides it from the pane (unless *Show archived* is on) and stops new posts: *It is hidden from the list and takes no new posts.* **Unarchive collection** brings it back. **Delete collection** permanently deletes the collection and its schedules; its posts move to the Default collection unchanged. You confirm by typing its name. |

The Default collection has no Danger zone: it can be renamed, but not archived or deleted.

A collection's icon can be set through the API (`PATCH /v1/collections/{id}`), not in this
drawer.
