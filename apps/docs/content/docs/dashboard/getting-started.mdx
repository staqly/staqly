---
title: Getting started
description: The welcome wizard that takes a new account from its first sign-in to its first post, once.
---

Right after you create an account, and for every new workspace, Staqly opens a short **welcome** on
its own page, without the sidebar. It takes about a minute. The steps only collect your answers;
nothing is saved until the end. On Staqly Cloud the last step is the workspace's plan, and the
workspace is set up and opens once the payment (or Starter's trial, card required) is through. With a
plan already in place, **Skip for now** (top right) ends it at any point.

## The steps

| Step | What you do |
|---|---|
| **Welcome to Staqly** | Say what Staqly should call you (shown in the sidebar and to your team) and what brings you here: posting as a creator, running a team, or automating with agents. |
| **Name your workspace** | Most people use their brand or client. You can rename it later in **Settings**. |
| **Where do you post?** | Pick the platforms your posts go to. You connect them in the last step. **I'll do it later** moves on. |
| **Connect an agent** or **Invite your team** | Which one comes first follows your answer in the first step. For an agent, pick Claude Code or Codex; its API key (it can write and publish posts) is made at the end and put into the one-line command (shown only then). Invitations take several addresses at once and a role, and are sent at the end. |
| **Choose a plan** (Staqly Cloud, a workspace without a plan) | Starter's 7-day free trial (card required, nothing charged until it ends) or any plan, through Stripe. |
| **You are all set** | The workspace is set up: renamed, people invited, the agent's key shown. Connect the platforms you picked (X and the other OAuth platforms bring you back here; **Your own automation** needs only a name), then **Go to the calendar** or **Write your first post**. |

People who join through an invitation see a shorter version: their name, the workspace they joined
and what their role lets them do.

## When it shows

- **Cloud:** once per person, right after the first sign-in of a new account or an accepted
  invitation. Finishing or skipping it ends it for good; it is stored with the person, so it doesn't
  come back on another device.
- **Staqly Community Edition:** never. The Community Edition has one workspace and no team to invite, so its empty
  pages (Schedule, Posts, Channels) guide you instead.
- It opens only from the dashboard's home (the calendar). A link to a post, a preview or Settings
  opens what it points to.
- **Getting started** in the user menu (bottom left, cloud only) opens it again whenever you like.

Over the API, `GET /v1/session` reports `onboarded`, and `POST /v1/session/onboarded` (from the
dashboard) finishes it; see the [API reference](/api/reference).
