**Settings** is where you manage the workspace itself, rather than its content: its name and
look, which browsers and phones can sign in, the job queue, exports and backups. Open it
from the workspace menu or the user menu (both at the edges of the sidebar), or from the command
palette (<Kbd>⌘</Kbd> <Kbd>K</Kbd>).

## Common tasks

- **Connect your phone**: **Settings → General → Devices → Connect a device** (on a local
  install also in the user menu). In cloud mode it signs the phone in as you. See
  [Phones & tablets](/dashboard/phones-and-tablets).
- **Sign out a lost device**: find it under **Devices** and sign it out.
- **Back up your data**: **Settings → Backups** shows the commands for Staqly Community Edition
  (`staqly serve backup <file>`, or `docker exec staqly server backup /data/backups/<file>` with
  Docker); on Plus and above it creates and lists downloadable backups (on Starter it points to
  the data-only export). See
  [Backup & restore](/self-hosting/backup-and-restore).
- **Download everything**: **Settings → Data & privacy → Export workspace** (on the Starter plan
  without the media files).
- **Check your channels**: **Settings → System → Channels** shows the platforms the server can
  connect and tests each connected account with **Test** or **Test all**.
- **See what the server is doing** (local installs): **Settings → System → Queue** lists publishing
  and schedule jobs with a **Retry** for failed ones.
- **Connect an agent**: open **Integrations** (under *Automation* in the sidebar) for copy-ready
  setup steps.
- **Change how you sign in**: **Settings → Account → Sign-in methods** (cloud). See
  [Accounts](/accounts#sign-in-methods).

## Settings tabs

Settings has these tabs: **General**, **Members** (cloud, for owners and admins), **Account**
(cloud), **Plan & usage** (with the billing in cloud mode), **Data & privacy**,
**Backups** and **System**. Alerts have their own page, **Alerts** in the sidebar (see
[Alerts](/automation/alerts)).

### General

| Section | What it does |
|---|---|
| **Workspace** | Rename the workspace and pick its **Time zone** (searchable; a new cloud workspace takes your browser's zone, the Community Edition the machine's). Repeating posts keep their time of day in this zone across daylight saving changes, and new schedules start in it. Save keeps both. |
| **Appearance** | Light, dark or system theme. |
| **Devices** | Browsers and phones signed in to this workspace, with when each was last seen. **Sign out** any of them (it loses access at once and has to be connected again); **Connect a device** creates a new QR code. Signing a browser in again with a new link replaces its old entry, so each browser is listed once. |
| **Sign out** <Badge>cloud</Badge> | **Log out** ends the session in this browser. |

How long a post on [your own automation](/channels/your-own-automation) may wait for a result is a
collection setting (**Fail publishing if nothing is reported within** in
[Collection settings](/dashboard/collections#collection-settings)); without one, the server's
`PROCESSING_TIMEOUT` (default 1 hour) applies.

Every browser signs in once, also in the Community Edition (with the access token, a link or a QR
code), so **Devices** lists every browser that signed in, this computer included.

### Members and Account (cloud)

**Members** lists who can open the workspace with their role, and the open invitations.
**Invite people** opens a short form (an address and a role). Plans are priced per user: while the
workspace pays, the card says how many seats are billed and the form says what one more person
costs ("Each person you add is billed €29/month on Plus (prorated)."). **Account** shows your
**Profile** (your name), your sign-in methods (add Google, add a passkey, change your email address,
each in a small dialog) and your **Workspaces** with your role in each (owners add new ones). See
[Accounts](/accounts). Its
**Notifications** card picks which events reach you by email and in the bell; see
[Your notifications](/dashboard/notifications).

### Plan & usage

The workspace's plan and where it stands (trial with the days left, active, payment failed,
ending), this month's usage, and in cloud mode for owners and admins the billing: the price per
user, the seats and the next payment, add-ons, extra X posts, the card on file and the invoices.
**Change plan** opens the plan picker. See [Plan & usage](/dashboard/plan-and-usage).

### Data & privacy

| Section | What it does |
|---|---|
| **Activity log** | Download every event, oldest first, as JSONL (one JSON event per line, the full event shape; `.jsonl`) or CSV. For a filtered slice, use **Export** on the [Activity](/dashboard/activity) page. |
| **Export workspace** | Build a ZIP with collections, posts, channels (without credentials), schedules, events, media files and webhooks. The card shows the progress; download it within 24 hours. API keys, sessions, the recovery key and channel credentials are never included; webhook secrets are redacted. |
| **Retention & single posts** | A pointer to deleting one post (**Delete** in the post) and to server-side retention of old events and deliveries. Erasing a post together with its history is a purge over the API (`POST /v1/posts/{id}/purge`, admin; see [Data export](/self-hosting/data-export#purge-a-post)). |
| **Delete workspace** <Badge>cloud</Badge> | Permanently delete the workspace and everything in it, and sign out every device. Only a signed-in dashboard session can do this, and you confirm by typing the workspace name. Earlier backups still contain the data. |
| **Delete all data** <Badge>Community</Badge> | The Community Edition has one workspace, so there is no delete button. The card shows how to remove the data instead: `staqly service uninstall` and `rm -rf ~/.local/share/staqly` with the binary, or `docker rm -f staqly` and `docker volume rm staqly-data` with Docker. |

In cloud mode on the Starter plan, **Export workspace** builds a data-only ZIP: the same files
without the media files (`media.ndjson` still lists each file by id, name and type). A note
under it says that files, backups and the activity log export come with Plus and above, and the
**Activity log** card is not shown.

See [Data export](/self-hosting/data-export).

### Backups

In the Community Edition, the **Backup & restore** card shows the commands for a consistent copy of
the database and the uploads, in two tabs:

- **staqly serve**: `staqly serve backup ~/staqly-backup.db` (pass the same `--data` you run
  Staqly with), then copy the `uploads` folder of the data directory.
- **Docker**: `docker exec staqly server backup /data/backups/staqly-backup.db`, `docker cp` to
  copy it out, and a `tar` of the uploads from the data volume.

In cloud mode on a paid plan, the **Backups** card lists the workspace's backups
(`GET /v1/workspace/exports`). **Create backup** builds a ZIP of the whole workspace
(collections, posts, channels, schedules, events and media); when it is ready, **Download**
works for 24 hours. On Starter the tab says that backups come with Plus and above and links to the data-only
export in **Data & privacy**.

See [Backup & restore](/self-hosting/backup-and-restore).

### System

| Section | What it does |
|---|---|
| **Health** | Live readiness of the staqly API (*Ready* or *Not ready*, the version, and each check: database, migrations, background workers), refreshed every 30 seconds, with a **Check now** button. |
| **Channels** | The platforms this server can connect (their app keys are set) and every connected channel with its state and last error. **Test** asks the platform whether the account still signs in and shows the answer; **Test all** checks them one after another. A channel that no longer signs in turns *Needs reconnect*. |
| **Queue** (local installs) | The background jobs: **Queued**, **Running**, **Failed** and **Done today** counts, a filter (all, failed, queued, running) and the latest jobs with their kind (*Publish a post*, *Publish on a channel*, *Run a schedule*, *Refresh a channel sign-in*), status, tries and last error. **Retry** queues a failed job again; the **×** cancels a queued one. Refreshes every few seconds. See [Jobs](/concepts#jobs). |
| **Monitoring** (local installs) | The monitoring endpoints with your instance's API URL: `/livez`, `/readyz` and `/metrics` (Prometheus; needs `METRICS_TOKEN` except for `curl` on the same machine in the Community Edition). |

See [Monitoring](/self-hosting/monitoring).

## Integrations

**Integrations** (under *Automation* in the sidebar) has these tabs: **Setup** (how to connect an
agent), **Agents & activity** (who is connected and what they did) and, in cloud mode, **API keys**.

On **Setup**:

- **Connection**: your instance's agent URL and a **Test connection** button (it checks
  `/health` and your session through the dashboard proxy and shows the version, workspace
  and who you are signed in as). The Community Edition points agents at this computer with its
  **access token** over HTTP (or **STDIO** inside the Docker container, without one); cloud mode
  points them at its own address with an API key. The Community Edition never uses cloud API keys.
- **Access token** <Badge>Community</Badge>: the token agents, scripts and n8n send as
  `Authorization: Bearer`, masked, with **Copy** and **Rotate**. Rotating makes a new token: the old
  one stops working at once, every other browser is signed out (this one stays) and your agents need
  the new token. See [the access token](/local-vs-cloud#the-access-token).
- Tabs **Claude Code**, **Codex**, **OpenCode**, **n8n** and **REST & MCP**, each with a
  copy-ready snippet using your instance's real URL (with `$STAQLY_API_KEY` for the access token
  locally, and in cloud mode the API key you just created) and a **Full guide** link to these docs. `?tab=codex` opens a tab directly;
  `?view=agents` opens Agents & activity.

On **Agents & activity**:

- **Agents** (MCP clients) and **API connections** (REST clients): everything that talked to this
  workspace (see [Agents](/dashboard/agents)).
- **Recent activity**: the latest agent events (in cloud mode: events triggered with API
  keys). Select one to see only its events; **Show all** goes back.
  **View all** opens [Activity](/dashboard/activity).

**API keys** <Badge>cloud</Badge> (`/integrations/api-keys`):

- **Create API key**: a name (required), a set of [scopes](/api/authentication#scopes) and
  an expiry: 1 hour, 8 hours, 1 day, 1 week, 1 month (the default), 1 year, or a day you pick
  up to a year ahead. A key never lasts longer than a year. Presets fill in the scopes for you:

  | Preset | Scopes |
  |---|---|
  | **Content agent** (default) | `collections:read`, `posts:read`, `posts:write`, `posts:publish`, `media:read`, `media:write` |
  | **Publisher automation** | `posts:read`, `posts:publish` (reports results for your own automation) |
  | **Read-only agent** | `collections:read`, `posts:read`, `channels:read`, `events:read` |
  | **Full access** | Every scope, including `admin` |

  Only **Full access** can delete: tick `posts:delete` (posts and media) or
  `collections:delete` yourself for a key that should clean up.

  The raw key is shown **once**.
- Each key shows its state: *Active*, *Expired* or *Revoked*.
- **Rotate** a key: revoke it and issue a replacement with the same name and scopes; the new
  key is shown once.
- **Revoke** a key.
- API keys are for agents and automation: people sign in to the dashboard with Google, an email
  code or a passkey, never with a key. In a workspace without people, the key from signup,
  **Admin key** (or **Admin key (recovered)** after a recovery), is listed here too; don't revoke
  your last admin key unless you still have the recovery key.

Snippets on the integration pages include the key you just created. It is kept only in memory
in that browser tab, never in local storage.

## Webhooks

**Webhooks** has its own sidebar entry: create, edit, test and delete endpoints, and browse
the delivery history with manual retry. Each webhook is one row that starts with its
**Active** / **Paused** switch (a paused webhook sends nothing; events in the meantime are
skipped). The timeout is set in seconds. Click a delivery to see its full error and the
receiver's response. See [Webhooks](/automation/webhooks).

## Forms and messages

These conventions hold across the dashboard (posts, collections, channels, schedules,
webhooks, alerts, API keys):

- **Forms** open in a drawer on the right; short ones (a name, an address, a code) in a dialog.
  Closing a drawer with changes you haven't saved (Esc or **×**) asks **Discard your changes?**
  first; after a save it closes without asking.
- **Required fields** are marked with a red `*` after their label.
- **Invalid fields** turn red, with the message right under the field. Server-side errors
  (for example a name that is already taken) appear on the field they belong to.
- **Messages** (toasts such as *Webhook created* or an error) appear at the bottom right and
  close on their own; each has a close button (**×**) to dismiss it right away.
