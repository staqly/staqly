The Inbox is where you review your agents' work. Every **pending** item (anything an agent
saved that still needs a decision) lands here, from every
[collection](/concepts). Open the Inbox whenever you want to work through that
queue.

It is the dashboard's start page: open **http://localhost:8080** (or
**http://localhost:8080/inbox**).

## Steps

The bar on top follows an item through its life, left to right: **Review** (waiting for you), **Approved** or
**Rejected** (nothing has run yet), **Processing** (your automation works on it, named after the action:
Publishing, Regenerating), then **Completed** or **Failed**. **Expired** (nobody decided before the item's
expiry) and **Archived** sit at the end. Each step shows how many items are there; click one to list them.
Review is the default and keeps the shortcuts and oldest-first order; the other steps list the newest changes
first.

**Filter** narrows any step by collection, source (the agent, n8n, a watcher), tags and type.

On wide screens each row shows the title with one small line under it (the item's AI summary, or the start of
its content until there is one), then columns: **Tags**, **Collection** (the source as a small badge under it),
the time (**Added** while reviewing, **Waiting** on Processing, **Decided** elsewhere, with a `v2` badge for
later versions), **Status** and **Actions**. Outside Review, a row's button is its collection's main action
(Publish, Retry...); the rest are in the item's side panel.

Ticked rows get a bar at the bottom: Reject and Approve while reviewing, the collection actions that apply
elsewhere, **Unarchive** on Archived, and **Delete** everywhere. Archived rows have an **Unarchive** button: the
item goes back to where it was. An item archived while it waited for review goes back to **Review**; otherwise
it returns to its run, else its decision (`POST /v1/items/{id}/unarchive`).

## Review your queue

<Steps>
<Step>

### Pick an item

The Inbox lists pending items oldest first, each with its title, the start of its content, the collection,
the agent that added it and when. Most items can be decided right from the list with **Reject** and
**Approve** on the row. Click a row (or move with <Kbd>j</Kbd> / <Kbd>k</Kbd> and press <Kbd>o</Kbd>) to open
the whole item in a side panel: preview, details, edit, history and every action.

</Step>
<Step>

### Approve it

Press <Kbd>⇧</Kbd> <Kbd>A</Kbd> or click **Approve**. The item leaves the list at once and a toast offers
**Undo** for 10 seconds; only then is the approval sent, the item becomes `approved` and webhooks receive
`item.approved`. Undo puts the item back in the list as if nothing happened. The approval waits in that browser
tab, so closing the tab within those 10 seconds drops it.

To fix something first, press <Kbd>⇧</Kbd> <Kbd>E</Kbd> (**Edit & approve**): change the title, the
content (or the JSON) or the URL, add an optional note, then approve. The edit and the
decision are saved as one change.

</Step>
<Step>

### Or reject it with feedback

Press <Kbd>⇧</Kbd> <Kbd>R</Kbd> or click **Reject** and say what is wrong ("too dark", "wrong
product"). Feedback is required (up to 750 characters). It is stored on the item and sent to your automations, so an
agent can regenerate the item. Tick **Ask the agent to regenerate with this feedback** to start the
collection's Regenerate right away.

</Step>
<Step>

### Or run a custom action

Items can also offer [custom actions](/automation/custom-actions) such as *Publish*,
*Regenerate* or *Archive*. Which ones appear depends on what the item's collection defines for
its current status. Actions that start a run (Publish, Regenerate) come after a decision: on an
item waiting for review you approve or reject first. They ask for confirmation in a dialog (*Publish "Summer
sale"?*) that says which event your automation receives; actions that need feedback (Regenerate) ask for it
instead, and Archive runs right away.

</Step>
</Steps>

When two people (or agents) decide the same item at the same moment, exactly one decision
wins. The other one sees a conflict instead of silently overwriting it.

## Try it without an agent: send a test approval

When nothing is pending, the Inbox shows *Inbox zero* with two buttons:

- **Send a test approval** adds a sample text item titled *Test approval* (source
  `staqly-local-test`, or `staqly-cloud-test` on a cloud instance, metadata `{"sample": true}`) to the workspace's **Default** collection
  and opens it. Approve or reject it to see
  how decisions, history and webhooks work. It counts as a normal item, so webhooks see it too.
- **Connect an agent** opens [Integrations](/dashboard/settings#integrations).

## Decide many items at once

1. Select items with <Kbd>x</Kbd> or their checkboxes. <Kbd>Shift</Kbd>-click a checkbox to
   select every item between it and the last one you clicked, like in Gmail. The checkbox in
   the list header selects or clears all loaded items, and the header shows the count
   (*3 to review*, or *2 of 3 selected*).
2. In the bar at the bottom click **Approve** (or press <Kbd>⇧</Kbd> <Kbd>A</Kbd>), or **Reject**
   (<Kbd>⇧</Kbd> <Kbd>R</Kbd>) to reject them all with the same feedback. <Kbd>Esc</Kbd> clears
   the selection.
3. Confirm. Approving several items asks *Approve 3 items?* first, and the reject dialog says how many items
   it rejects. Neither has an Undo: to edit an item before approving it, open it on its own.

On the other steps you can select items too: the bar then offers the collection actions those
items allow (for example **Publish** or **Archive**), **Unarchive** on Archived, and
**Delete**, never approve or reject.

Up to 100 items are processed per request, and each one succeeds or fails on its own. If
someone else already decided an item, it is reported and the rest go through.

## Decision and run

Every item shows two separate things, never mixed in one badge:

- **Decision** (outlined badge): what the reviewer decided: *Pending*, *Approved*, *Rejected* or *Expired*.
- **Run** (tinted badge, only when something ran): what your automation did after it: *Processing* (spinning
  while it works), *Completed*, *Failed* or *Canceled*. A new decision starts without a run.

On **Processing**, each row shows how long the item has waited of its processing limit (a bar; the time it
fails on its own on hover). When your automation never reports back, report the result by hand from the row's
small menu: **Completed**, **Failed** or **Canceled**. Canceled is stored as a failed run with the error
*Canceled in the dashboard* and shown as *Canceled*.

*Archived* is a third, separate mark. The API returns the same as `decision`, `run` and `archived_at` next to
the older single `status`, and `GET /v1/items?decision=approved&run=processing,failed` filters by both.

## Filter the list

| Filter | Options |
|---|---|
| **Step** (the bar on top) | *Review* by default (oldest first, with review shortcuts), *Approved*, *Rejected*, *Processing*, *Completed*, *Failed*, *Expired*, *Archived* |
| **Collection** (Filter) | All collections, or one |
| **Source** (Filter) | The agent, n8n or watcher that added the item |
| **Tags** (Filter) | Items with any of the picked tags |
| **Type** (Filter) | `image`, `video`, `text`, `link`, `json`, `file` |

Filters live in the URL (`?step=failed&collection=col_oalirbz67y4m4cqxq5dw&type=image`), so you can bookmark a
view such as "failed images in Instagram Avatar". The list refreshes on its own every few seconds.

## Keyboard shortcuts

| Keys | Action |
|---|---|
| <Kbd>j</Kbd> or <Kbd>↓</Kbd> | Next item |
| <Kbd>k</Kbd> or <Kbd>↑</Kbd> | Previous item |
| <Kbd>⇧</Kbd> <Kbd>A</Kbd> | Approve the selected items, or the current one |
| <Kbd>⇧</Kbd> <Kbd>R</Kbd> | Reject with feedback (the selection, or the current item) |
| <Kbd>⇧</Kbd> <Kbd>E</Kbd> | Edit, then approve |
| <Kbd>⇧</Kbd> <Kbd>P</Kbd> | Outside Review: run the row's main action (Publish, Regenerate, Retry...), through the same dialog as its button |
| <Kbd>x</Kbd> | Toggle selection |
| <Kbd>⇧</Kbd> + click | Select a range |
| <Kbd>o</Kbd> or <Kbd>Enter</Kbd> | Open the item in the side panel |
| <Kbd>Esc</Kbd> | Clear selection |
| <Kbd>?</Kbd> | Show shortcuts |
| <Kbd>⌘</Kbd> <Kbd>K</Kbd> / <Kbd>Ctrl</Kbd> <Kbd>K</Kbd> | Command palette (anywhere) |

Decisions always need <Kbd>Shift</Kbd>, so a stray key never approves or rejects anything.
On the steps other than Review the decision shortcuts give way to <Kbd>⇧</Kbd> <Kbd>P</Kbd>, and the
legend lists only what applies. Shortcuts are ignored while you type in a field or while a dialog is open. In the feedback
box, <Kbd>Enter</Kbd> submits and <Kbd>Shift</Kbd> + <Kbd>Enter</Kbd> adds a new line.

## Review on a phone

On small screens the Inbox shows **one large card at a time**. Swipe left and right (or tap
the arrows) to move between items, and use the action bar at the bottom: *Reject*,
*Approve*, and a menu for custom actions. Filters sit behind the **Filters** button. To
connect your phone, see [Phones & tablets](/dashboard/phones-and-tablets).

## Content from the internet

Items created by [watchers](/automation/watchers) carry `metadata.untrusted = true` and
show an **External content** badge. Treat their text and links as untrusted.
