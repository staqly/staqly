The **Assistant** (in the sidebar, or <kbd>⌘</kbd> <kbd>J</kbd>; the sparkles on a phone) opens a chat on the right
of the dashboard. Ask in your own words:

- "Write a LinkedIn post about our new pricing and schedule it for Monday 9:00"
- "What is scheduled this week?" or "Which posts failed, and why?"
- "Create a collection for Instagram that posts every weekday at 9:00"
- "Move all drafts tagged *launch* to the Launch collection"

It works with the same tools AI agents get over [MCP](/agents), as you and with your role: a viewer's assistant can
only look. Looking things up happens at once. **Every change waits for you**: a card says what it is about to do
(the post's text, the time, the channels) with **Allow** and **Deny**. Posts it writes are drafts unless you ask
for a time or to publish now.

Your chats are kept on the server, yours alone in each workspace: **Chats** (the clock) lists them newest first,
searched by title, and opens one where you left it, a change still waiting for Allow included; **New chat** starts
another. The panel's left edge drags to make it wider, and **Full screen** gives the chat the whole window (Esc
goes back). What you type and what the tools find
(post texts, names) go to the AI service to answer; secrets don't (it has no tools that show API keys, webhook
secrets or channel tokens, and none that connects a channel or deletes the workspace).

## Where it runs

On the server's AI provider, like the [design editor's AI tools](/dashboard/design-editor#where-ai-runs): staqly
cloud includes it. On your own server, either:

- `AI_FAL_KEY`: fal.ai's router, where `AI_ASSISTANT_MODEL` picks any OpenRouter model (Gemini by default, or
  `anthropic/claude-sonnet-4.5`, `openai/gpt-5`...), or
- `AI_ASSISTANT_BASE_URL` (+ `AI_ASSISTANT_API_KEY`, `AI_ASSISTANT_MODEL`): any OpenAI-compatible API, such as OpenAI,
  OpenRouter, DigitalOcean's serverless inference, a local Ollama or your own LiteLLM.

See [configuration](/self-hosting/configuration#ai-tools). Without either the Assistant is not shown. Each answer
counts as AI usage of the workspace (`AI_ASSISTANT_PRICES` when the endpoint reports no cost).

### When it can't answer

`AI_ASSISTANT_FALLBACK_*` sets a second, usually cheaper model. It answers when the workspace's monthly AI
allowance has run out, and when the first model refuses before answering (its own quota ran out, it is down). A
model that already started answering is not replaced halfway.

When neither can answer and the server names a model for the device (`AI_ASSISTANT_BROWSER_MODEL`), the chat offers
**Continue on this device**: the assistant then runs in your browser (it needs WebGPU: Chrome, Edge, Safari 26 or
later). It is free and nothing leaves the browser, but it only writes text: it can't read or change anything in the
workspace. The first time downloads the model (a few GB); New chat goes back to the server.

### Pictures and videos

Ask for a picture or a video ("make a 9:16 video of a sunrise over the city") and the assistant makes it with the
server's AI tools (`generate_media`), after you allow it. A picture takes about a minute, a video a few; it then
offers to keep the result in the media library, ready for a post.
