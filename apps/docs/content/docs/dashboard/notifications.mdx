In a cloud account every person decides what Staqly tells them about. **Settings → Account →
Notifications** lists each event with two switches: **Email** (to the address you sign in with)
and **Bell** (the notifications in the dashboard's user menu). Save once for the whole card.

| Event | Who gets it | Default |
|---|---|---|
| Post published | the post's author, and everyone who follows every post | email and bell on |
| Post failed to publish | the post's author, and everyone who follows every post | email and bell on |
| Feedback on a preview | the post's author, and everyone who follows every post | email and bell on |
| Channel needs reconnecting | owners and admins | email and bell on |
| Schedule out of posts | owners and admins | email and bell on |
| Someone joined | owners and admins (not the person who joined) | email and bell on |

**Posts to tell me about** picks between **My posts** (the default) and **Every post in my
workspaces** (editors, admins and owners). A post's author is the person who created it; a post
an [API key](/api/authentication) or an agent created belongs to the person who made that key, so
your agent's posts reach you as "my posts". Posts from a schedule's endpoint have no author and
reach only the people who follow every post.

## The bell and the Notifications page

The bell (**Notifications** in the sidebar, the bell on a phone) shows only what is new since you last looked;
closing it or **Mark all as read** clears the count. **All notifications** at its foot opens the
**Notifications** page: every notification of the workspace, newest first and by day, with when it happened (date
and time), the workspace, the channel, who or what did it, the event's id (to copy), and links to the post, to
the post on its platform, and to the same event in [Activity](/dashboard/activity). **Load older** goes further
back.

## The emails

One email per post and person, whatever the number of channels: *Your post was published on X
and My publisher* with the post's first line, a row per channel with **Open on X** links, and **View
post** in the dashboard. *Publishing failed on X* shows each channel's error. Every email ends
with **Change notification preferences** and **Unsubscribe from these emails**: the link turns
off that one kind of email at once, without signing in. Mail clients that offer a one-click
unsubscribe button use the same link (`List-Unsubscribe`).

These notifications are part of [Staqly Cloud](/local-vs-cloud#staqly-cloud). Staqly Community
Edition has no accounts: use an [email alert](/automation/alerts) there.

## Notifications and alerts

These settings are **yours**: they follow you across the workspaces you're in. An
[alert](/automation/alerts) belongs to the **workspace**: it sends every matching event to one
place (a team chat, an ntfy topic, a shared inbox) for everyone who reads it. Use both: alerts
for the team's channel, your notifications for what you personally care about.

## API

`GET /v1/account/notifications` and `PUT /v1/account/notifications` (a signed-in person, not an
API key) read and change the choices; `POST /v1/notifications/unsubscribe` is what the email's
link calls. See the [API reference](/api/reference).
