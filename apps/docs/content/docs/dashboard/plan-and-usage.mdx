---
title: Plan & usage
description: Check your plan, its trial or renewal, this month's usage, add-ons, the card on file and the invoices.
---

**Plan & usage** shows which plan your workspace is on, how close you are to its limits and, on
[Staqly Cloud](/local-vs-cloud#staqly-cloud), its billing. Check it when something stops working with a "limit reached" error
(HTTP `402`), or before adding more channels, webhooks or API keys. In Staqly Community Edition there are
no limits and no billing: the plan is always *Unlocked*.

Open **Settings → Plan & usage**, **Plan & usage** (or **Billing**, for those who manage it) in the user menu, or search for *Plan & usage* in the
command palette (<Kbd>⌘</Kbd> <Kbd>K</Kbd>). Everyone in the workspace sees the plan and the usage;
the billing (next payment, add-ons, card and invoices) and its buttons are for owners and admins,
and paying is for the workspace's owners.

## The plan

The top of the page names the plan (*Starter*, *Plus*, *Pro* or *Business*) and where it stands:

| Status | What it means | Buttons |
|---|---|---|
| *Trial · N days left* | Starter's free 7-day trial (card required), once per workspace and one workspace per owner at a time. It counts down to the end of the trial; the card on file is charged then. | **Manage billing**, **Change plan** |
| *Active* | A subscription is paid. | **Manage billing**, **Change plan** |
| *Payment failed* | Stripe could not charge the card; the plan runs until the end of the paid period plus a few days. | **Manage billing** |
| *Ends* a date | The subscription is set to stop and will not renew. | **Manage billing**, **Change plan** |
| *Read-only* | The trial or paid period ended unpaid. The rest of the dashboard is locked until a plan is paid for here. | **Choose a plan** |
| *No plan yet* | A new workspace before its owner finishes the welcome and its plan. | **Start free trial** |

Under it: the **price** per user (excl. VAT), the **seats** (every member is billed) and, for
owners and admins, the **next payment** (e.g. *€87 on Nov 10, 2026*, *3 users × €29*).

## Usage

One meter each for this calendar month (UTC): *Posts this month* (resets on the 1st),
*Channels*, *Webhooks*, *API keys*, *Storage* and *Alerts*, each against its limit (∞ when
unlimited). A meter shows *Almost full* above 80 % and *Limit reached* at the limit; a limit raised
by an add-on says so (*15 + 10 from Channels pack*). Platforms whose posts have an allowance of
their own (X: its API charges per post) are lines under **Per platform** inside *Posts this month*,
each with its own bar; posts you buy come on top of it. Below the meters:

- **Posts created this period**, counted by their current status. Each count opens the posts list
  with that filter;
- **Webhook delivery**: deliveries *Queued* for their first attempt, *Retrying* after a failure,
  and *Failed* after every retry (redeliver them from **Webhooks**);
- the largest file you can upload on the plan.

## Add-ons and extra X posts (cloud)

- **Extra X posts**: the posts bought on top of the plan's monthly X posts, how many are left and
  when they expire. **Buy more X posts** (owners) opens the bundles (200 for €10, 1,000 for
  €40, 4,000 for €150, excl. VAT) and Stripe Checkout; they appear when Stripe confirms the payment.
- **Add-ons** (owners and admins): each with its price per month or year. **Add** charges the rest
  of the billing period now; **Remove** stops it and credits the unused rest on the next invoice.

## Payments (cloud, owners and admins)

The page reads Stripe each time it opens. Staqly stores no card data.

- **Payment method**: the cards on file (brand, last four digits, expiry), the default one marked.
  **Update** opens the Stripe billing portal.
- **Invoices**: every invoice Stripe has for the workspace, newest first, 24 at a time (**Show older
  invoices** loads more), with a link to Stripe's invoice page (**View**, or **Pay** for an open one)
  and the PDF. When payments go through Stripe Managed Payments, Link sells the
  subscription and emails the receipts and invoices; the page says where to find them.

## Change plan

| Where | How |
|---|---|
| **Choose a plan**, **Change plan** | Opens the plan picker with a **Monthly / Yearly** switch. Without a subscription, a plan opens Stripe Checkout; with one, it switches the subscription at once (Stripe prorates). After paying you come back to **Plan & usage**; the plan changes as soon as Stripe confirms (a toast says *Payment received* or *Plan changed*). Paying needs an owner of the workspace. |
| **Manage billing** | Opens the Stripe customer portal: change the card, download invoices or cancel. Shown to owners once the workspace has a Stripe customer. |

Every plan can download a workspace export in **Settings → Data & privacy**. On Starter it is
data only, without the media files; backups, the export with the files and the activity log
export come with Plus and above. See [Data export](/self-hosting/data-export).

See [Plans & limits](/plans) for what each plan includes.

## Through the API

`GET /v1/workspace/usage` (every member) answers the plan and the usage; `trial_ends_at` is when the
trial ends (`plan_expires_at` adds a few days of payment grace to a Stripe-billed period).
`GET /v1/billing/details` (scope `admin`) answers the subscription, the cards and the invoices
(`starting_after` pages them). See the [API reference](/api/reference).

```bash
curl -s -H "Authorization: Bearer $STAQLY_API_KEY" http://localhost:3333/v1/workspace/usage
```
