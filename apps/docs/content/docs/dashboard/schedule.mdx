title: Schedule
description: See every scheduled post and upcoming schedule run on a calendar, move posts by dragging them, and add a post into a free slot.

**Schedule** is the home page and the publishing calendar: every post with a time, by day and
hour, in your time zone, together with the upcoming runs of your collections' schedules. It
has its own entry in the sidebar (and the phone tab bar) and in the command palette
(<Kbd>⌘</Kbd> <Kbd>K</Kbd>).

## The calendar

| View | What you see |
|---|---|
| **Day** | One day with an hour grid. |
| **Week** | Seven days with an hour grid. Each post is a chip with the collection colour as its left bar (grey without one), the time, the first line of the text, its channels as small avatars and its state as an icon: a clock for *Scheduled*, a spinner for *Publishing*, a check for *Published*, a triangle for *Failed*. Past hours are hatched up to the line that marks the current time. |
| **Month** | A month at a glance, up to two chips per day; **+N more** opens that day. |
| **List** | The same range as a plain list grouped by day (the toggle next to the view switch; phones always use it). |

Upcoming **schedule runs** show as hollow chips: `09:00 · Weekday mornings · next post`. They
say what will go out at that time without naming a post yet, because the schedule picks it when
it runs. Click one to open the collection's schedules.

The header carries the count and the time of the visible range as a subtitle, with the
controls centred on the title row: previous, **Today**, next, the range, then the view toggles.
The view, the layout and the day, week or month are kept in the URL, so a link opens the same
page. The grid scrolls on its own, with the day headers and the hour axis staying put, and
opens around the current hour.

Click a post to open its [sheet](/dashboard/posts#the-post).

## Add a post into a slot

Future slots have a **+** (shown when you hover an hour or a day, always on a touch screen).
It opens **Schedule a post here** with every draft in your collections (search them with
**Search drafts**): pick one and it is scheduled for that time on its channels. **New post for
this time** at the bottom opens the composer with the publish time already set. Past slots have
no **+** and take no drop.

## Reschedule or clear

Drag a chip to another day (month view keeps the time of day) or to another hour (week view) to
move it; a toast confirms the new time. In the post itself, the calendar button in the footer
(**Schedule for later**) picks a new time, and the **×** on the time chip clears it; saved without
a time, the post is a draft again.

Only drafts, scheduled and failed posts can be moved. Once a post is publishing or published
its time is read-only; a failed post publishes again with **Publish now**, or one channel with
**Retry**.

## Time zones and history

Times are stored in UTC and shown in the browser's time zone; the picker names it. Schedules
keep their own zone (a 09:00 in `Europe/Warsaw` stays 09:00 across daylight saving changes); a
new schedule starts in the workspace's time zone (**Settings → General → Workspace**). The post's
history in [Activity](/dashboard/activity) records *Scheduled* or *Rescheduled* with the time,
*Schedule cleared*, and *Published* or *Failed* per channel.

API: `POST /v1/posts/{id}/schedule`, `POST /v1/posts/{id}/cancel`,
`GET /v1/posts?publish_after=&publish_before=&sort=publish_asc` for the calendar, and the
`post.scheduled` / `post.unscheduled` events; `GET /v1/schedules` for the runs.
