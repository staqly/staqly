**Channels** in the sidebar lists every connected account with its picture, name, handle,
platform and status, and is where you add new ones. For what a channel is and what each
platform allows, see [Channels](/channels).

## Add a channel

<Steps>
<Step>

### Pick the platform

Click **Add channel**. The drawer shows the platforms in groups: **Your own**, **Social
networks**, **Video and streaming**, **Communities and chat**, **Blogs and newsletters** and
**Business and portfolio**: *Pick the platform. Greyed ones are on the way.* Platforms that are
not ready on this server carry a **Soon** badge and cannot be picked: not built yet, or an OAuth
platform whose app keys the server doesn't have. Platforms turned off on the server
(`PROVIDER_<ID>_ENABLED=false`) are not shown at all. **Your own automation** is always available
unless it is turned off the same way.

</Step>
<Step>

### Connect

- **Token platforms** show their credential fields (a bot token and chat id for Telegram, a
  handle and app password for Bluesky) and a **Name**. **Connect** checks the credentials
  against the platform and saves the channel with the account's name and picture.
- **OAuth platforms** show **Connect with ...** (the platform's name), which sends you to the
  platform and back. The platform's developer app lives in the server's environment
  (`PROVIDER_<ID>_CLIENT_ID` and `_CLIENT_SECRET` in `.env`), locally and in the cloud; until it
  is there the platform shows **Soon**. See [Developer apps](/channels/developer-apps).
- **Your own automation** takes a **Name** and nothing else: *One account, one channel. You can
  connect more later.*

</Step>
</Steps>

After the platform sends you back, you land on **Channels** with the new channel in the list, or
on *The channel was not connected* with the platform's error and **Back to Channels**.

## The list

Each row shows the channel's picture, name and status, its platform and handle, and its
activity: **Last post** (how long ago, linking to that post), **Next post** (the next scheduled
one) and how many posts went out on it in the last 30 days, or *No posts yet*. Hover a time for
the exact date.

**Groups.** Every channel is in one group, for example one group per client; the **Default**
group always exists. With only Default, the list is grouped by platform. **New group** (next to
Add channel) creates another; from then on the list shows one section per group, each folding open
and closed, with a **group filter** above it. A group's menu has **Rename** and, for every group
but Default, **Delete group**: its channels move to Default after you confirm.

| Status | Row |
|---|---|
| **Connected** | Ready to publish. |
| **Needs reconnect** | Shown in amber with the last error. Posts targeting it fail until it is reconnected. |
| **Disabled** | Greyed; it is not offered in the composer and schedules skip it. |

Each row's menu has:

| Action | What it does |
|---|---|
| **Settings** | A drawer with the channel's name (the handle stays the account's), its **Group** (when there is more than one) and **Links in posts**: keep links, remove them or show them as plain text (X offers only the last two). See [Channels](/channels#managing-channels). |
| **Move to group** | Puts the channel in another group (shown when there is more than one). |
| **Test connection** | Checks the stored credentials against the platform now; a failing test marks the channel *Needs reconnect*. |
| **Disable** / **Enable** | Turns the channel off or on without removing it. |
| **Reconnect** | OAuth platforms go through the platform again; token platforms take fresh credentials. |
| **Remove** | Removes the channel. The confirmation warns: *Scheduled posts lose this channel.* Posts with no other channel become drafts. |

## Where channels show up

- As avatars on top of the composer, with a character counter and a tab per picked channel.
- In a schedule's **Channels**.
- On calendar chips and post rows as a stack of avatars; in the post sheet with a status per channel.
- Plan limits (cloud): **5 channels on Starter**, 15 on Plus, 40 on Pro, unlimited on Business. **Settings → Plan & usage** counts
  them.

## Let someone else connect a channel

The **link** icon next to **Add channel** makes a [connect link](/channels/connect-links): a client
or a colleague without access to Staqly opens it, signs in at the platform and the channel lands
in your workspace, in the group you picked. Open links are listed in the same dialog with
**Revoke**.
