The design editor makes graphics for posts: a quote card, an announcement, a story, a carousel, a YouTube
thumbnail, or a photo touched up for a channel. It works like pages you arrange: pick a size, add text, photos and
shapes, and save to **Media**. It runs in your browser.

## Open it

- **Design** at the top of **Media** starts a new design.
- **Edit** in a picture's drawer, or on pictures you selected in Media, opens them as a page of the first one's
  size. A picture saved by the design editor opens as its design again, at its page, with everything editable.
- In a post, the pencil on an attached picture opens it in a new tab. Save it there and the post uses the result.
- Projects of the former photo editor can't be edited any more: their saved pictures open as new designs.
- **Design** under a post's text opens a new design in a new tab. Save it there and the post gets the picture;
  saving again later replaces it in the post.

## Make a design

The left bar holds the tools:

- **Templates**: ready-made designs (quote, announcement, sale, event story, tips, testimonial, YouTube
  thumbnail) and your saved designs. A template replaces the page; **Undo** brings your page back.
- **Text**: a heading, subheading or body text, or a styled one: click it to add it in the middle, or drag it
  onto the page where you want it. Double-click a text to type in it. The bar above
  the page sets the font (13 fonts, served by staqly itself), size, colour, bold, italic, underline, alignment,
  letter spacing, line height, an outline, and **Box**: a coloured box behind the text with padding and round
  corners.
- **Photos**: upload pictures (or drop or paste them onto the editor), pick them from your library, or, when the
  server has an Unsplash key, search Unsplash. Uploaded pictures go to your library too. With a photo selected,
  the bar above the page offers:
  - **Crop** (or double-click the photo): drag the photo inside its frame, zoom with the slider or the mouse
    wheel. Dragging a side handle of the frame crops too; the photo always fills its frame.
  - **Shape**: cut the photo to a circle, arch, hexagon, triangle, star or heart.
  - **Remove background**: done on your device, nothing is uploaded. The first use downloads the model (about
    200 MB) from Hugging Face; your browser keeps it for next time. The cut-out goes to your library too.
  - **Adjust** (brightness, contrast, saturation, blur, black and white), **Corners** and flips.
- **Elements**: squares, rounded squares, circles, triangles, stars, lines and arrows; click or drag them onto the
  page. A shape is filled with a colour or a gradient, like the background.
- **Background**: a colour or a gradient (two or three colours, along an angle or out from the centre), or the
  selected photo stretched over the page.
- **AI**: describe a picture and AI makes it, or select a photo and tell AI how to change it ("put this product on
  a marble table"). Shown only when the server has an AI key (see below).
- **Layers**: the order of everything on the page, top first: drag a layer up or down (or focus its handle and
  use the arrow keys), show/hide and lock.
- **Size**: a page size per channel (Instagram feed and stories, TikTok, YouTube, X, LinkedIn, Facebook,
  Threads, Pinterest, Open Graph) or your own. With **Fit the content to the new size**, the design scales and
  stays centred.

The bar under the page holds the **pages**: add one, duplicate it, move it left or right, delete it. Several pages
make a carousel (a post people swipe through on Instagram, LinkedIn, Threads, X or TikTok); every page has its
own background and elements, and they share the size.

Every element can be made see-through, given a shadow, brought to the front or sent to the back, locked,
duplicated and deleted. Under **Look**, a photo or a shape can also fade to see-through towards one side, over
the length you choose. Elements snap to the page's edges and centre and to each other; pink lines show where.

| Keys | |
|---|---|
| Ctrl/Cmd+Z, Ctrl/Cmd+Shift+Z | Undo, redo |
| Ctrl/Cmd+D | Duplicate |
| Ctrl/Cmd+A | Select everything not locked |
| Arrows (Shift: 10 px) | Move the selection |
| Delete, Backspace | Delete the selection |
| Esc | Select nothing |

## Save

**Save to Media** saves every page as a picture (JPG when it has photos, PNG otherwise; "Name (2 of 3)" for a
carousel) and keeps the design itself, so it opens again editable. Saving again replaces the pictures; posts that
use them follow. Opened from a post's **Design** button, a new design's pages are added to that post.
**Download** saves the page on screen, or every page, as PNG or JPG without touching the library. The Save
button shows a dot while there are unsaved changes; going back to Media then asks first.

A design with Unsplash photos reminds you whom to credit when you save.

## AI pictures

The AI tools run on the server's OpenAI account: its operator sets `AI_OPENAI_API_KEY`, and optionally
`AI_OPENAI_FALLBACK_API_KEY` for when OpenAI refuses or fails on the first key, and `AI_OPENAI_MODEL`
([configuration](/self-hosting/configuration)). Workspaces never see or set a key. Without one, the **AI** tool
is not shown.
